## Q1. Zella AI: capabilities, grounding, UI placement, AI reviews, Zella Insights, AI Agents, credit metering, MCP connector, reception

### Takeaway
Zella AI is a credit-metered, chat-first assistant grounded only in the user's own journal data (trades, strategies, notes, plus TradeZella's market data and, since 29 Sep 2026, its help-center knowledge base) that was first teased as "ZellaAI" in April 2025, documented as a chat assistant with 500/1,000 monthly credits under the old two-tier pricing, relaunched as "Your AI Trading Partner" on 26–29 May 2026, extended with three scheduled agents (Market Sentiment Briefing/Game Plan, Trade Auto-Tagger, Session Review) announced live on 22 Jul 2026 and gated to Pro/Ultra, and exposed to external LLM clients through a remote MCP server (`https://mcp.tradezella.com/mcp`, beta 6 Oct 2026, submitted to the Claude and OpenAI connector directories 20 Oct 2026). Credits (500/1,500/3,000 per month on Essential/Pro/Ultra) are spent per chat message and per agent run, reset monthly, and cannot be topped up; automated backtest runs are metered separately.

## Q2. Strategies (formerly Playbooks): rule groups, conditional rules, Executed/Missed/Sample trades, statistics and rule-adherence scoring, template gallery and creators, caps, links to trades and backtests

### Takeaway
A Strategy is a named, iconized setup definition made of free-named rule groups whose rules can be shown Always or only for Winner/Loser/Break-even trades; users (or the Auto-Tagger) link trades to a strategy and tick the rules followed, which yields per-rule Follow rate, Net P&L, Profit factor and Win rate and per-strategy Expectancy/Win rate/Profit factor; missed trades can be logged by symbol and hypothetical time, "sample" trades illustrate shared templates and are cited by Guided Backtesting, a public gallery of creator-authored templates (added as "Playbook 2.0/Playbook Templates" in Apr–May 2025) can be duplicated or backtested in one click, and Essential is capped at 10 strategies while Pro/Ultra are unlimited.
