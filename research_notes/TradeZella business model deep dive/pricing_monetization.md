## Key question 1: Current plans, prices, features and limits (late 2026)

### Takeaway
Since 14 July 2026 TradeZella sells three paid tiers and nothing free: Essential $35/mo or $315/yr, Pro $59/mo or $531/yr, and a new Ultra tier at $99/mo or $891/yr; annual billing is a uniform 25% discount, and the tiers are differentiated mainly by number of connected accounts, strategy/playbook count, trade-replay and backtesting quotas, AI credits and mentor invites.

### Cited Findings
- TradeZella's help-center article "Our Pricing" describes the July 2026 update: Essential rose from $29/mo to $35/mo monthly and from $288/yr to $315/yr annually; Pro from $49/mo to $59/mo and from $399/yr to $531/yr; Ultra is new at $99/mo or $891/yr. (snippet) — [TradeZella Help Center: Our Pricing](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/8911582-our-pricing)
- TradeZella's own blog post on the new pricing confirms the three tiers and prices ($35/$59/$99 monthly; $315/$531/$891 annually). (snippet) — [TradeZella blog: New Pricing, Every Plan Explained (2026)](https://www.tradezella.com/blog/tradezella-pricing)
- Essential (per help center): one active account, 10 strategies, and 10 trade replays per month without the 1-second chart. (snippet) — [TradeZella Help Center: Our Pricing](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/8911582-our-pricing)
- Essential has no automated backtesting, though "one free run is included." (snippet) — [TradeZella blog: New Pricing](https://www.tradezella.com/blog/tradezella-pricing)
- Pro: up to 50 accounts, unlimited strategies and replays, 1-second charts and AI insights, 1,500 AI credits and 10 automated backtest sessions per month. (snippet) — [PurePowerPicks TradeZella review 2026](https://purepowerpicks.com/tradezella-review/); consistent with [TradeZella blog: New Pricing](https://www.tradezella.com/blog/tradezella-pricing)
- Ultra: everything in Pro plus unlimited accounts and unlimited mentor invites, 3,000 AI credits and 100 automated backtest sessions per month. (snippet) — [TradeZella blog: New Pricing](https://www.tradezella.com/blog/tradezella-pricing); [PurePowerPicks review](https://purepowerpicks.com/tradezella-review/)
- A September 2026 third-party review lists Essential's AI allowance at 500 credits/month and describes the offer as paid-only (no free plan). (snippet) — [Traders Second Brain: TradeZella Review 2026](https://traderssecondbrain.com/guides/tradezella-review); the 500-credit figure was not seen in TradeZella's own pages.
- Third-party reviews describe annual billing as "about 25 percent less" than monthly ($315/$531/$891 vs $35/$59/$99 × 12). (snippet) — [Kagels Trading: TradeZella Review 2026](https://kagels-trading.com/blog/tradezella-review/); [Quantum-Algo: TradeZella Review 2026](https://www.quantum-algo.com/blog/guides/tradezella-review/)
- TradeZella's own comparison page now lists Essential, Pro and Ultra tiers, confirming the tier names have changed more than once (Basic/Premium → Basic/Pro → Essential/Pro/Ultra). (snippet) — [TradeZella vs Tradervue](https://tradezella.com/vs/tradervue)
- The Zella AI assistant is documented in a help article and is the feature that consumes "AI credits." (snippet) — [TradeZella Help: What is Zella AI](https://intercom.help/tradezella-4066d388d93c/en/articles/11201153-what-is-zella-ai-tradezella-s-ai-trading-assistant)
- Mentor Mode (mentors see students' synced broker data, dashboards, trade logs, and leave feedback on trades) is a built-in feature whose invite limits vary by plan; one comparison lists "Mentor Invitations: 5 mentees" on the entry plan and Ultra includes unlimited mentor access. (snippet) — [TradeZella Mentor Mode page](https://www.tradezella.com/mentor-mode); [TradeZella vs Tradervue](https://www.tradezella.com/vs/tradervue)
- Prop Firm Sync is labeled "COMING SOON" on one TradeZella page and "NEW" on another; a third-party guide says it is free on every plan. (snippet) — [TradeZella Review 2026 (tradezella.com blog)](https://www.tradezella.com/blog/tradezella-review-2026-honest); [TradeZella tutorial for beginners](https://www.tradezella.com/blog/tradezella-tutorial-beginners); [DamnPropFirms TradeZella review](https://damnpropfirms.com/trading-tools/tradezella/)

### Inferences
- Arithmetic on the cited prices: Essential $315/yr = $26.25/mo (exactly 25% off $420); Pro $531/yr = $44.25/mo (25% off $708); Ultra $891/yr = $74.25/mo (25% off $1,188). The annual discount is therefore a uniform 25% ("3 months free") on every tier, replacing the old asymmetric discounts (see Q2).
- Monthly price ratios are 1 : 1.69 : 2.83 (Essential : Pro : Ultra); Ultra at $99 functions as a price anchor that makes $59 Pro look mid-range.
- The packaging ladder gates by usage volume (accounts, strategies, replays, backtests, AI credits) rather than by feature families, which lets TradeZella upsell multi-account prop-firm traders and mentors/educators into Ultra.
- No add-on SKUs (extra AI credits, extra accounts, extra seats) were found in any source; everything appears bundled into the three tiers. Treat this as "not found," not "confirmed absent."

### Gaps
- Could not read the live pricing page, so exact FAQ wording, badge labels, and the full feature matrix (e.g., exact Essential AI credits, replay/backtest definitions, Pro account limit of 20 vs 50) remain partially unverified; sources conflict on Essential's AI credits (500 in one review; absent from TradeZella snippets).
- Could not confirm whether any add-on purchases (AI credit top-ups, additional accounts) exist.

## Key question 2: Pricing history 2020–2026 and grandfathering

### Takeaway
TradeZella's list prices were flat from its 2022 commercial launch until 14 July 2026 (Basic $29/mo or $288/yr; Premium—later renamed Pro—$49/mo or $399/yr), after which it raised monthly prices ~20%, raised Pro annual 33%, added Ultra, and grandfathered existing subscribers at their old price only as long as they make no plan or billing-cycle change.

### Cited Findings
- Pre-July-2026 pricing: Basic $29/mo or $288/yr (≈$24/mo); Premium/Pro $49/mo or $399/yr (≈$33.25/mo); annual Basic saves $60 (17%), annual Premium saves $189 (32%). (snippet) — [StockBrokers.com TradeZella review](https://www.stockbrokers.com/review/tools/tradezella); [DayTradingZ TradeZella review](https://daytradingz.com/tradezella-review/); [TradeZella Pricing Guide 2026 (tradezella.com blog)](https://www.tradezella.com/blog/tradezella-pricing-guide-2026)
- TradeZella's own 2026 pricing guide (written before the July change) lists "Basic $29/month, $24/month ($288/year)" and "Pro $49/month, $33.25/month ($399/year)" and says Pro opens up Trade Replay, full backtesting, and up to 20 accounts. (snippet) — [TradeZella Pricing Guide 2026](https://www.tradezella.com/blog/tradezella-pricing-guide-2026)
- Pre-2026 Basic limits were generally described as one trading account and three playbooks, with Pro/Premium adding unlimited playbooks and more accounts. (snippet) — [DayTradingZ review](https://daytradingz.com/tradezella-review/); [StockBrokers.com review](https://www.stockbrokers.com/review/tools/tradezella)

## Key question 4: Discounts, promo codes, seasonal sales, student/prop-firm discounts, affiliate and referral programs

### Takeaway
TradeZella's discounting is channel-driven: a tiered Partner (affiliate) program paying 20–30% of subscription revenue for 12 months (plus 10% lifetime at Gold/Top) whose Silver+ partners get a personalized 20%-off code (hence the many "XX20" coupons online), a credits-based referral program now paused for restructuring, and a Black Friday promotion (2025 code BF25F, reported as 50% off) restricted to Annual Pro; no student, military or prop-firm discounts were found.

### Cited Findings
- Partner Program tiers (help center): Base (no requirement); Bronze — refer 1 paid customer, 20% for 12 months, 1 free month of TradeZella; Silver — 10 paid customers, 30% for 12 months, personalized 20%-off coupon for your audience, beta feature access; Gold — 100 paid customers, 30% for 12 months plus 10% lifetime commission, free lifetime TradeZella account, priority support; Top — 400 paid customers, same commission structure as Gold plus free lifetime account. (snippet) — [TradeZella Help: Partner Program](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/12467249-tradezella-partner-program); tier table matches [TradeZella Partners page](https://www.tradezella.com/partners) ("earn up to 30% commission for a full year")
- Commissions stop if a referred customer cancels, receives a refund, or disputes the payment; promotion is allowed on YouTube, TikTok, X, Instagram, Discord, blogs and newsletters subject to brand/compliance guidelines. (snippet) — [TradeZella Help: Partner Program](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/12467249-tradezella-partner-program)
- Referral Program (credits, not cash) "is being restructured, so it's not available at the moment"; last version had four tiers — Launch (0 referrals), Bronze (1), Silver (3), Gold (5); the first successful referral earned $50 in TradeZella credits; reaching Gold led to an invitation into the Partner Program; progression counted only "Successful Referrals," not pending leads. (snippet) — [TradeZella Help: Referral Program](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/12467249-tradezella-referral-program) (same article ID as the Partner Program page, implying the article was renamed/rewritten)
- TradeZella is hiring an "Affiliate Partnerships Lead." — [TradeZella careers: Affiliate Partnerships Lead](https://careers.tradezella.com/o/affiliate-partnerships-lead)
- Babypips (large forex-education site) runs a TradeZella partner page. — [Babypips partners: TradeZella](https://partners.babypips.com/tradezella)
- Black Friday 2025 (official help article): code BF25F applies to Annual Pro via Settings → Subscription → Change Plan/Upgrade; eligible: monthly Basic or Pro users upgrading to Annual Pro, new sign-ups, and expired users renewing to Annual Pro; excluded: active Annual Basic and active Annual Pro plans, early renewals/extensions; cannot be stacked on an existing annual plan; a code fails if applied to an unsupported plan type or has expired. (snippet) — [TradeZella Help: Black Friday Discount Guide](https://intercom.help/tradezella-4066d388d93c/en/articles/12875345-black-friday-discount-guide)

- No 2026 Black Friday code had surfaced as of 9 Nov 2026 in search results.
- Evergreen 20%-off affiliate codes in circulation: WOR20 (snippet) — [Steemit coupon post](https://steemit.steemapps.com/tradezella/@alexbrianblog/tradezella-coupon-20-off-professional-trading-analytics-2025); YAS20 (snippet) — [Coda coupon page](https://coda.io/@alexbrian/tradezella-promo-code-yas20); MM20 "Save 20% on TradeZella" (snippet) — [Modest Money day-trading category](https://www.modestmoney.com/category/day-trading); DGT (snippet) — [DamnPropFirms: TradeZella review with discount code DGT](https://damnpropfirms.com/trading-tools/tradezella/); generic coupon aggregator — [Wethrift TradeZella](https://www.wethrift.com/tradezella)
- No TradeZella student, military or first-responder discount appeared in any result; the search for one returned only unrelated companies.
- No TradeZella–prop-firm commercial partnership (Topstep, Apex, etc.) was found; results covered the firms separately.

### Inferences
- The "XX20" codes (WOR20, YAS20, MM20, DGT) are almost certainly the Silver-tier "personalized 20% off coupon," meaning TradeZella's de-facto street price for influencer-sourced customers is 20% below list (e.g., Pro annual $531 → ~$425).
- Affiliate unit economics at current prices: a Silver partner referring one Annual Pro subscriber earns ~$159 (30% × $531) in year one; Gold/Top partners additionally earn ~$53/yr for life on that subscriber (10%). Combined with a 20% customer discount, first-year net revenue to TradeZella on an influencer-sourced Annual Pro is roughly $425 − $127 commission ≈ $298 (≈56% of list), which is still comparable to a direct Essential annual sale.
- Restricting Black Friday to Annual Pro (not Essential, not Ultra, not existing annual plans) targets conversion of monthly and lapsed users into 12-month prepaid mid-tier commitments without discounting the installed annual base.

### Gaps
- Affiliate cookie window, payout schedule/threshold, and the tracking platform (Impact, PartnerStack, Rewardful, Tolt, FirstPromoter, etc.) were not found; the Babypips partner page terms could not be read.
- Black Friday discount size is based on a single Slickdeals report; the official article (snippet) did not state the percentage. Earlier years' (2022–2024) Black Friday offers were not found.
