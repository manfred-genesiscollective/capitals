## Key Question 1: Who founded TradeZella, what did they do before, and what is the origin story?

### Takeaway
TradeZella was founded by Umar Ashraf, a New York-raised, Pakistan-born options/day trader and YouTube trading educator (Stock Market Lab, Ashraf Capital) who says he lost his first $20,000 at 18 and later built a journaling product because existing tools did not meet his needs; Fast Company (Oct 2024) reports he bootstrapped it, had "false starts" before bringing development in-house, and employs two of his sisters, who third-party databases list as co-founders heading marketing and product.

## Key Question 2: When did the company form and launch, and what is the dated 2020-2026 milestone timeline?
