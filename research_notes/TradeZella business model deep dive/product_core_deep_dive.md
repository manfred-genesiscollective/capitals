## Product map, naming history and 2026 tier gating (cross-cutting context needed to read every section)

### Takeaway
By August 2026 the app has a two-level navigation (top level: Home · Journal · Backtesting · Agents · Mentor Mode (BETA) · PropFirm Sync; inside "Journal" at `/tracking`: Dashboard, Day View, Trade View, Notebook, Reports, Strategies, Trade Replay, Progress Tracker, Resources), which replaced the Feb-2025 "modes" side panel (Tracking / Backtesting / Mentor / University) and the original "Trade Tracking page" with Day / Week / All tabs; help-center articles still use the older names (All tab = Trade Log page = Trade View; Playbook = Strategy). Since July 14, 2026 the plans are Essential $35, Pro $59, Ultra $99 per month with account limits of 1 / 50 / unlimited active accounts.

## Dashboard: widget catalogue, definitions, calendar P&L rules, views/filters, customization flow, Zella Score formulas

### Takeaway
The dashboard has an upper "key metrics" strip (max 5 widgets) and a lower "visual insights" area, editable through saved "Templates" and a "Chart Library" (added May 2024); widgets are documented with definitions (Net P&L on closed trades only; Day Win % = winning days ÷ trading days; Current Streak = combined winning-trade and winning-day streak; Drawdown computed from cumulative P&L in $ view and from running balance incl. deposits/withdrawals in % view); the calendar books P&L on the exit day (full or partial), and the Zella Score is a weighted composite of six 0–100 sub-scores whose win-rate, average-win/loss, max-drawdown and (partially) recovery-factor scoring rules are published, while the profit-factor and consistency rules are not visible in any retrievable source.

### Cited Findings
**Layout and customization**
- The dashboard "is divided into two sections: Upper Section: Designed for key performance metrics. Lower Section: Designed for more detailed and visual insights. The Upper Section allows up to 5 widgets at a time." Upper-section widget options include Net P&L, Trade Win %, Profit Factor, Day Win %, Trade Expectancy, streak widgets, Max Drawdown and Average Drawdown; Zella Score is listed among lower-section widgets; the lower section also has a "Recent Trades & Open Positions" widget. — [Understanding Dashboard Widgets and Stats (article 7118437)](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/7118437-understanding-dashboard-widgets-and-stats) (snippet; search metadata dates its last update to ~Aug 2025); [Unrealized P&L FAQ (article 12572470)](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/12572470-unrealized-p-l-faq) (snippet)
- Customization flow: click the "Template" button in the top right → "Edit" (pencil icon) or "Create New Template" → add, remove or rearrange widgets → save; "You can create and save multiple templates depending on what stats you want to monitor." An older flow in the same article: Edit Widgets → Edit Section → select widgets and drag to reorder. — [How to Add / Edit Dashboard Widgets (article 9718410)](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/9718410-how-to-add-edit-dashboard-widgets) (snippet)
- May 2024 release: "select from the Chart Library and hit 'Insert to Dashboard'" to add/remove/rearrange widgets. — [What's NEW: May 2024](https://www.tradezella.com/blog/whats-new-may-2024) (snippet)
- Hovering the (i) icon on any widget shows that metric's definition. — [Getting started with TradeZella (article 13863136)](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/13863136-getting-started-with-tradezella) (snippet)
- First-hand Aug 2026 observation of the populated default dashboard: Net P&L · Trade win % (semicircular gauge) · Profit factor (donut) · Day win % (gauge with win/breakeven/loss day colours) · Avg win/loss trade (horizontal bar, avg win left/green, avg loss right/red) · Zella score (hexagonal radar with six axes Win %, Profit factor, Avg win/loss, Recovery factor, Max drawdown, Consistency, plus a 0–100 number on a red→yellow→green gradient bar; 58.79 with 9 trades) · Daily net cumulative P&L (line with fill) · Net daily P&L (green/red bars) · Recent trades / Open positions (tab switch) · Account balance (two series: balance and Deposits/Withdrawals) · Drawdown (inverted red-filled area) · Calendar (monthly grid, each day coloured with $ amount and trade count). Top bar: units switch · Filters · Date range · All accounts · "View my day" · "Last import: … Resync". The tester reported the layout as fixed ("no Layout/Sections customization"). — [UI tour](https://github.com/0xsickre/Trading_Journal/blob/eee601fbc49e4292730d5b1c77d26b4c03d7fad6/docs/tradezella-ui-tour.md) — **conflicts with** the help-center Template/Edit-widgets flow above; the tester may not have opened the "Template" control.
- A Progress Tracker widget can be added to the dashboard; it "displays a daily score and a heatmap, and darker shades indicate more rules followed that day." — [How to Add the Progress Tracker Widget to Your Dashboard (article 10352075)](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/10352075-how-to-add-the-progress-tracker-widget-to-your-dashboard); [Getting Started with the Progress Tracker (article 10352042)](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/10352042-getting-started-with-the-progress-tracker) (snippets)

**Widget definitions**
- Day Win % = "the share of winning trading days out of all trading days"; Current Streak = "a combined view of winning trade and day streaks"; Current Day Streak = "the count of consecutive winning trading days". — [article 7118437](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/7118437-understanding-dashboard-widgets-and-stats) (snippet)
- Net P&L headline "is based on closed trades only"; floating/unrealized P&L "isn't shown for imported trades and isn't included in any statistics"; "There's no account-wide floating P&L or floating drawdown. Instead, a Drawdown widget tracks day-level drawdown, and per-trade running P&L appears on the Tracking page when you open a trade's details." — [Unrealized P&L FAQ (article 12572470)](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/12572470-unrealized-p-l-faq) (snippet)
- Drawdown widgets: "drawdown is calculated based on cumulative PnL in $ view, and based on account running balance (including deposits, withdrawals, and cumulative PnL) in % view"; "The Max Drawdown widget tracks the deepest single decline, while the Drawdown widget tracks cumulative declines over time"; the Average Drawdown widget "reflects the average drawdown across the selected timeframe or current drawdown if no filter is set." — [Understanding Drawdown Widgets in TradeZella (article 8427754)](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/8427754-understanding-drawdown-widgets-in-tradezella) (snippet)
- The Account Balance widget requires balance adjustments entered in account settings (2023-era release note); the current path is three dots next to the account → "Edit Balance" → "Add Adjustment" (deposits/withdrawals) after setting the initial balance. — [April–May release notes](https://www.tradezella.com/blog/april-may-release-notes) (snippet); [Setting Your Account Balance (Deposit/Withdrawal Amounts) (article 7236181)](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/7236181-setting-your-account-balance-deposit-withdrawal-amounts-in-tradezella) (snippet; last updated ~Mar 2025)
- Known issue logged Dec 2024: the playbook filter "did not affect Current Streak, Current Day Streak, or Day Win %". — [Upvoty changelog](https://trade-zella.upvoty.com/f/changelog/) (snippet)

**Views (units) toggle**
- Seven dashboard views: Dollars (default), Percentage, Privacy ("hides sensitive data like account balance and profit/loss"), R-Multiple ("displays data based on the initial risk entered for trades"), Ticks ("for futures trades, showing stats in ticks price movement"), Pips ("displays data for forex trades in pips"), Points ("for futures trades … Stats in points price movements"); switched via the "View" button on the dashboard top bar. — [Dashboard Views in TradeZella (article 9858998)](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/9858998-dashboard-views-in-tradezella) (snippet); Pips and Points were announced as new alongside the "7 different dashboard options" — [TradeZella on Threads](https://www.threads.com/@tradezella/post/DChU8lFysoN?hl=en); pips described as a way "to compare your performance across different currency pairs" — [September 2024 Release Notes](https://www.tradezella.com/blog/whats-new-september-2024) (snippet)
- In-app the menu annotates each unit: Dollar ✓ default; Percentage; Privacy; R-multiple "shown for trades with entered initial risk only"; Ticks "for future trades only"; Pips "for forex trades only"; Points "for future trades only". — [UI tour](https://github.com/0xsickre/Trading_Journal/blob/eee601fbc49e4292730d5b1c77d26b4c03d7fad6/docs/tradezella-ui-tour.md)
- Percentage view: "P&L is expressed relative to the starting balance, with daily figures based on that day's opening balance and weekly and monthly figures based on the first day of the period. Percentage View requires an initial balance on the account." — [Dashboard Percentage View (article 12451049)](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/12451049-dashboard-percentage-view) (snippet)

**Calendar**
- Daily P&L is booked "on the day a trade is closed or partially exited, not the day it was opened. Open positions add nothing to that day's P&L"; example: a trade opened Aug 10 and fully closed Aug 12 shows on Aug 12; a partial exit on Aug 14 shows that portion on Aug 14. — [How the Dashboard Calendar Calculates and Shows Daily P&L (article 10528734)](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/10528734-how-the-dashboard-calendar-calculates-and-shows-daily-profit-loss-p-l) (snippet; last updated ~Feb 2025); corroborated by "a partial profit is counted on the day it was taken, and a full close is counted on the day the position was fully closed" — [Why Does My Daily Journal P&L Not Add Up… (article 9887513)](https://intercom.help/tradezella-4066d388d93c/en/articles/9887513-why-does-my-daily-journal-p-l-not-add-up-compared-to-individual-trades) (snippet); contrasted with broker calendars (Moomoo) that measure daily change in account value — [article 13509677](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/13509677-why-does-my-p-l-look-different-between-moomoo-and-tradezella-calendars) (snippet)
- "Advanced Calendar Widget" (July 2024): shows monthly, weekly and daily stats; the weekly column shows "a running total of net profit and loss for each week" and "counts the days you traded in each week"; a note icon marks days with journal entries; colour coding distinguishes winning, losing and breakeven days; the user picks which stats appear per day: "R-Multiple, Day P&L, Ticks, # of trades, Day Win %". — [Advanced Calendar Widget in TradeZella Dashboard (article 9689020)](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/9689020-advanced-calendar-widget-in-tradezella-dashboard) (snippet); [July 2024 updates](https://www.tradezella.com/blog/new-in-july-2024) (snippet); [TradeZella on X, July 2024](https://x.com/TradeZella/status/1814390118461124620?lang=en)
- Reports also has a yearly "Calendar" tab (all 12 months as mini-calendars in a 3-column grid with prev/next year); a Jan 27, 2025 changelog entry fixed "the yearly calendar widget". — [UI tour](https://github.com/0xsickre/Trading_Journal/blob/eee601fbc49e4292730d5b1c77d26b4c03d7fad6/docs/tradezella-ui-tour.md); [Weekly TradeZella Updates – January 27, 2025](https://tradezella.canny.io/changelog/weekly-tradezella-updates-january-27-2025) (snippet)

**Zella Score (per-metric scoring)**
- Six metrics each scored 0–100, weighted and combined: Trade Win % 15%, Profit Factor 25%, Average Win/Loss 20%, Max Drawdown 20%, Recovery Factor 10%, Consistency Score 10%. — [Introducing the All-New Zella Score (article 10305642)](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/10305642-introducing-the-all-new-zella-score) (snippet; weight for Win % from the first-pass note on the same article; last updated ~Oct 2025 per search metadata)
- Trade Win %: scored as (win rate ÷ Top Threshold) × 100 with the default Top Threshold 60, so any win rate above 60% caps at 100 (worked example 25% → 41.67). — [article 10305642](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/10305642-introducing-the-all-new-zella-score) (snippet)
- Average Win/Loss bands: ≥2.6 → 100; 2.4–2.59 → 90–99; 2.2–2.39 → 80–89; 2.0–2.19 → 70–79; 1.9–1.99 → 60–69; 1.8–1.89 → 50–59; <1.8 → 20. — [article 10305642](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/10305642-introducing-the-all-new-zella-score) (snippet)
- Max Drawdown: "Max Drawdown % = (Largest Value Drop (Peak-to-Trough) / Max Cumulative P&L Before the Drop) × 100; Score Ranges: 100 − Formula Result". — [article 10305642](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/10305642-introducing-the-all-new-zella-score) (snippet)
- Recovery Factor "measures net profit against maximum drawdown"; published bands include 3.5 → 100, 3.0–3.49 → 70–89, 2.5–2.99 → 60–69 (lower bands not visible). — [article 10305642](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/10305642-introducing-the-all-new-zella-score) (snippet)
- Consistency Score: only fragments visible — "If average profit < 0, then 0" and "100 − Formula Result"; stated purpose "steady trading habits and reduces impulsive decisions". — [article 10305642](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/10305642-introducing-the-all-new-zella-score) (snippet)
- Launch: "Introducing the NEW Zella Score … LIVE ON 17th Dec, Tuesday" (Dec 17, 2024), replacing the 3-metric score (Win Rate, Avg win/loss, Profit factor) advertised in May 2024. — [TradeZella on X, Dec 2024](https://x.com/TradeZella/status/1867685776449814855); [TradeZella on X, May 2024](https://x.com/TradeZella/status/1790858565311172983); [What's NEW: December 2024](https://www.tradezella.com/blog/whats-new-december-2024)
- A third-party open-source clone states that TradeZella "does not publish the thresholds for profit factor, avg win/loss or recovery factor" — contradicted for avg win/loss and partially for recovery factor by the article snippets above; the clone's own formula is not TradeZella's. — [venkatalokeshanne/Journal lib/score.ts](https://github.com/venkatalokeshanne/Journal/blob/87e85a5e3edb2b761855b6b52d23193b3de21a61/lib/score.ts)

### Inferences
- The scoring design mixes two normalization styles — a linear ratio-to-threshold for win rate and discrete lookup bands for avg win/loss and recovery factor — so a faithful rebuild needs per-metric scoring tables, not one formula; the missing profit-factor table is probably banded like avg win/loss (inference only).
- "Day win %" with a three-colour gauge and "breakeven days" in the calendar legend imply a breakeven classification for days (and, per Reports, for trades); the threshold defining "breakeven" was not found.
- Because the Average Drawdown widget "reflects … the selected timeframe", the dashboard date-range control (seen first-hand) filters all widgets; no help article documents the date-range presets.

### Gaps
- Profit Factor score bands and the Consistency Score formula (fetch article 10305642 in full); whether the Top Threshold is user-editable in 2026.
- Date-range presets and the "Filters" / "All accounts" selector behaviour on the dashboard (only the control names were observed).
- Whether the "fixed layout" observation (Aug 2026) reflects a plan restriction, a UI change, or a tester oversight.

## Trade log / Trade View and the individual trade page (columns, filters, bulk actions, review workflow, trade-page layout, PT/SL and R fields, Zella Scale/MAE/MFE/Best exit, asset-class display)

### Takeaway
Trade View (help center: "All tab" / "Trade Log page") is a paginated table (50/page) with ten default columns and ~43 selectable ones, five filter groups (General, Tags, Day & Time, Strategy/Playbook, Zella Insights Beta) and a Bulk Actions menu (Mark as reviewed / not reviewed, Export to CSV, Merge/Split, Delete, Add to Strategy); the trade page is two-column with left tabs Stats · Strategy(Playbook) · Executions · Attachments and right tabs Chart · Notes · Running P&L, a header with "Mark as reviewed", Replay, Share and a gear holding chart settings and "Delete trade"; stop-loss/targets are entered post-hoc (since Feb 3, 2026 multiple PT/SL in six unit types) and drive Trade Risk, Initial Target, Planned and Realized R; MAE/MFE are rendered as a dollar "Zella Scale" bar and as "Best exit" columns, all computed from TradeZella's own price feed.

### Cited Findings
**Table, columns, pagination**
- Default All-tab columns: Open date, Symbol, Status, Close date, Volume, Net P&L, Net ROI, Zella Insights, Zella Scale, Reviewed; the table can be reshaped. — [Understanding the All Tab (article 7143872)](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/7143872-understanding-the-all-tab) (snippet)
- Summary strip above the table: Net cumulative P&L / Profit factor / Trade win % / Avg win-loss; checkbox column for bulk actions; "Trades per page: 50"; "Select columns" gear with All / None / Default shortcuts and this full list: Account name, Adjusted cost, Adjusted proceeds, Best exit (%), Best exit P&L, Best exit price, Best exit time, Close date, Close time, Commissions, Custom Tags, Duration, Entry price, Executions, Initial risk, Initial target, Instrument, Instrument type, Mistakes, Net P&L, Net ROI, Notes, Open date, Open time, Pips, Position MAE, Position MFE, Price MAE, Price MFE, Realized R Multiple, Return per pip, Reviewed, Setups, Side, Status, Strategy, Ticks per contract, Total fees, Total swap, Trade rating, Volume, Zella Insights, Zella Scale. — [UI tour](https://github.com/0xsickre/Trading_Journal/blob/eee601fbc49e4292730d5b1c77d26b4c03d7fad6/docs/tradezella-ui-tour.md)

**Bulk actions, export, edit, delete, transfer**
- Bulk actions on the All tab: select trades with checkboxes → "Mark as reviewed / Mark as not reviewed" ("flags trades so you can track which ones you've already gone through"), CSV export, merge/split. — [article 7143872](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/7143872-understanding-the-all-tab) (snippet)

## Progress Tracker: rule types (PREPARE / TRADE / REFLECT), evaluation, streaks, heatmap, reset, goals

### Takeaway
Progress Tracker (beta Feb 27, 2025; publicly announced Apr 23, 2025) scores each selected trading day against mandatory and custom rules grouped into Prepare / Trade / Reflect stages; progress counts only when "Finish My Day" is clicked, feeding a day streak and a GitHub-style heatmap; defaults include "Start my day by [09:30]", "Trading hours", "Link trades to playbook", "Input Stop loss to all trades", "Net max loss /trade" ($ or %), "Net max loss /day"; rule edits never rescore past days; idle days no longer break the streak (2025); an email reminder can warn before a streak is lost; "Reset all progress" wipes streaks, rules and achievements. No separate goals/targets feature was found.

### Cited Findings
- "Rules are organized into three trading stages, Prepare, Trade, and Reflect, and each stage includes both mandatory rules and any custom rules you add. Each rule is checked off during its matching part of the trading day"; "Rules only apply on the days you select as trading days. ## Mobile: native apps vs mobile web as of late 2026

### Takeaway
Despite a May 2024 company post promising iOS and Android apps "this year" and a handful of 2026 reviews that mention a "mobile app", the weight of evidence — including a review citing TradeZella's own statement as of Sept 22, 2026 — is that TradeZella remains web-only (responsive web/PWA) with no official App Store or Google Play listing; the web app blocks Trade Replay on mobile user agents with the message "this feature is not available in mobile version". The first pass's claim that native apps exist should be treated as unverified and probably wrong.
