# How TradeZella Turns Trader Discipline Into Revenue

TradeZella is a bootstrapped, self-described "highly profitable" trading journal that charges the highest prices in its category — **$35, $59 and $99 per month ($315, $531 and $891 per year) since July 14, 2026, with no free tier and no free trial** — and it sustains that position because distribution, not software, is the moat: founder Umar Ashraf's YouTube channel grew from roughly 86K subscribers in 2020 to about 900K by 2026, a tiered affiliate program pays creators 20–30% recurring commissions, and brokers such as Interactive Brokers and TradeLocker document the integration in their own help centers, so the company reports growing "mostly through word of mouth and social media" rather than paid ads ([Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91212604/built-by-one-of-their-own-tradezella-lets-day-traders-track-and-plan-transactions)). The product is an execution-level trade ledger (accounts → trades → fills) enriched asynchronously from TradeZella's own intraday price store (MAE/MFE, running P&L, "Zella Scale"), wrapped in review rituals (a reviewed flag, half-star ratings, Setups/Mistakes tags, Strategies with rule-adherence statistics, a "Start my day / Finish my day" loop with streaks) and, since 2026, an AI layer (Zella AI chat, three agents, plain-English automated backtesting, an MCP server) that is metered in credits and gated by tier. The bundle — "500+" claimed broker connections (in practice 16 auto-sync connectors, 25 CSV parsers and hundreds of MetaTrader server entries), tick-level trade replay, backtesting on data back to 2014, a free PropFirm Sync dashboard, Mentor Mode and Spaces — is what rivals cannot match at any single price point, even though every individual feature is matched or beaten by a cheaper competitor. Its weaknesses are equally concrete: price is the number-one complaint in critical reviews, roughly 37% of negative Trustpilot reviews cite bugs or broker-sync failures, a native mobile app promised for 2024 still did not exist in September 2026, AI usage is metered, exports cannot be re-imported and there is no API. Demand rests on a derivatives-heavy retail base that set records in 2025 (US listed options up 24% to 15.2 billion contracts; CME's retail futures traders up to 500,000+) and on prop-firm evaluations whose rules create a mechanical need for discipline tooling, while the inflow of brand-new investors has fallen sharply since 2021, so the market is intensifying rather than widening. For the team building capitals, TradeZella is best read as a proven data model and ritual design to copy, a trust posture (no trial, discretionary refunds, lock-in export) to exploit, and a warning about where the engineering money goes: broker connectors, historical market data and human support.

*Evidence note.* Direct fetches of tradezella.com, its Intercom help center, the Canny changelog, Trustpilot, app stores and the Wayback Machine were blocked in the research environment, so most facts below come from search-engine snippets of the cited pages and are labelled by source class (company claim, third-party review, competitor-written page, aggregator estimate). Three kinds of first-hand evidence anchor the product description: the user's own screenshot of a signed-in demo dashboard (November 2026), a detailed third-party walkthrough of a paid account dated August 22, 2026 published on GitHub (untrusted, but the only source that saw populated screens), and GitHub artifacts such as a saved copy of TradeZella's broker-support page, its generic import template and competitors' parsers for its export file. Where later deep-dive notes corrected first-pass findings, the later finding is used and the correction is stated: TradeZella has **no native iOS or Android app** (first-pass notes repeated 2026 reviews that mention a "mobile app"; TradeZella's own comparison page, a September 22, 2026 check and a user-agent-based "not available in mobile version" block establish that it is web-only); Pro's AI allotment is 1,500 credits after July 14, 2026 (the help center still shows the pre-repricing 1,000); the Essential plan gained Trade Replay only on August 4, 2026; and "Trade Replay 2.0" is a December 2025 relaunch of a 2023 feature. Revenue, subscriber counts and churn are not disclosed anywhere; every figure of that kind below is marked as an inference.

## A bootstrapped family company grew on a founder's 900K-subscriber audience

TradeZella's founder and CEO, **Umar Ashraf**, is a Pakistan-born, New York-raised options and day trader who built an audience before he built software. Benzinga billed him in May 2019 as founder of the education business Stock Market Lab and of "Ashraf Holdings Group LLC" ([Benzinga](https://feeds2.benzinga.com/news/19/05/13775202/founder-of-stock-market-lab-to-share-his-trading-secrets)); a wave of near-identical sponsored profiles in 2020–2021 listed Ashraf Capital, Stock Market Lab and TradeZella as his three companies, which dates the brand to at least **January 27, 2021** ([Haute Living](https://hauteliving.com/2021/01/umar-ashraf-founder-stock-market-lab-makes-name-world-trading/693989/)); and his podcast persona rests on a self-reported arc from losing $20,000 at 18 to "$18 million" in trading profits that no accessible source audits ([Chaindesk summary of WOR Podcast EP.70](https://chaindesk.ai/tools/youtube-summarizer/umar-ashraf-making-18-million-day-trading-wor-podcast-ep-70-_aZZkI13m6M)). Stock Market Lab was a high-ticket course with "more than 2,000" members by January 2021 ([Haute Living](https://hauteliving.com/2021/01/umar-ashraf-founder-stock-market-lab-makes-name-world-trading/693989/)); Trustpilot reviewers describe it as roughly $3,000 with "zero access to Umar" ([Trustpilot](https://jp.trustpilot.com/review/www.stockmarketlab.com)). Fast Company's October 2024 profile, the only independent journalism found, says Ashraf started the company after failing to find software that met his needs, that "false starts" led him to hire a team and bring development in-house with himself as CEO, that the software "first launched in early 2022," and that the business "was entirely bootstrapped and is now profitable (even employing two of Ashraf's sisters)" ([Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91212604/built-by-one-of-their-own-tradezella-lets-day-traders-track-and-plan-transactions)). The company's July 2026 pricing post confirms the launch year ("for the first time since launching in 2022") ([TradeZella blog](https://www.tradezella.com/blog/tradezella-pricing)), and a January 13, 2022 post records the moment it "started on-boarding the first batch of the users on the waitlist" ([TradeZella on X](https://twitter.com/TradeZella/status/1481419955023355905)). Third-party directories date formation earlier, to **March 2020 in Miami, Florida** ([The Trusted Prop](https://thetrustedprop.com/blogs/overview-of-tradezella-trade-copier); [StockBrokers.com](https://www.stockbrokers.com/review/tools/tradezella)), which reconciles with the 2021 brand mentions if 2020–2021 was a pre-launch, outsourced-build period; the legal entity name and state of incorporation were not found.

The team is small, remote and family-led. The careers page describes "a bootstrapped, remote-first team, highly profitable," hiring across engineering, design, content, support and marketing toward a mission of helping "1 million retail day traders achieve consistent profitability" ([TradeZella careers](https://www.tradezella.com/careers)). A contact-data aggregator lists **Saira Ashraf as "Co Founder + Head Of Marketing" and Khadija Ashraf as "Co-Founder / Head Of Product"** and estimates 21–50 employees while separately counting 13 across six departments ([Prospeo](https://prospeo.io/c/tradezella-email-format)); FINTECH.TV's November 2024 interview titles Ashraf "Co-Founder & CEO," corroborating that he is not the sole founder ([FINTECH.TV](https://www.fintech.tv/News/Detail/8899-the-power-of-trade-journaling%E2%80%94umar-ashraf-co-founder-ceo-explains-tradezella%E2%80%99s-mission)). Engineering has leaned on an outside studio: Railsware, a Ruby on Rails product studio, lists TradeZella as a client and has recruited Ruby/React engineers for a "trading-related project" it calls the "№1 trading journal" ([Wikiwand: Railsware](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Railsware); [Railsware vacancy](https://jobs.dou.ua/companies/railsware/vacancies/334887/)), while TradeZella itself advertised a US-remote Senior Full-Stack Engineer (Rails + React, PostgreSQL, Redis, background jobs) at **$150K–180K** around November 2025 ([Ruby on Remote](https://rubyonremote.com/jobs/70474-senior-full-stack-engineer-ruby-on-rails-react-at-tradezella)). No funding round or investor appears on Crunchbase or CB Insights ([Crunchbase](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/tradezella); [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/investor/tradezella)), and the only valuation figure, "valued near $100 million," is a May 2023 podcast blurb with no transaction behind it ([Everand](https://www.everand.com/podcast/648962611/UMAR-ASHRAF-28-Year-Old-Makes-18-Million-Day-Trading-Stocks)).

Traction is reported by the company in marketing numbers and by third parties in traffic and review counts, and the two must be read differently. The homepage claims **"100K+ traders" and "20.2B trades journaled"** ([TradeZella home-v2](https://www.tradezella.com/home-v2)), while the company's own 2026 review post says "over 50,000 active traders" and "20.5+ billion" trades ([TradeZella blog](https://www.tradezella.com/blog/tradezella-review-2026-honest)); the gap between 100K and 50K most plausibly separates cumulative sign-ups from active accounts, and 20 billion "trades" for 100K users (200,000 each) can only be an execution or imported-history count, so it is a vanity metric. An undated freelancer portfolio describes the product as a "Trading Journal With Over 30,000 Users" ([Contra](https://contra.com/p/mmoURFX2-trading-journal-with-over-30000-users)). Semrush estimates **about 1.0–1.1 million monthly visits (June 1.04M, July 1.13M, August 1.01M; year not displayed, most likely 2026), 71.8% direct, with a 9:44 average session** ([Semrush](https://semrush.com/website/tradezella.com/overview)), the profile of a logged-in web app whose users return directly rather than of marketing traffic. Trustpilot shows a **4.8/5 TrustScore with the review count rising from 621 (November 2025) to 835 (March 2026) to 1,055 (October 2026)** ([Gridinsoft](https://gridinsoft.com/online-virus-scanner/url/tradezella-com); [Trustpilot analysis](https://tradingjournalcom.netlify.app/blog/trustpilot-analysis)). The founder's YouTube channel, which Fast Company calls a promotional channel for TradeZella, grew from 86.1K subscribers in December 2020 ([Daily Times](https://dailytimes.com.pk/?p=696220)) to about 802K by December 28, 2024 ([vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/UCir8ZEhgIOLY__2tN1Pi0ig)) and roughly 917K on a 2026 tracker page ([SponsorRadar](https://sponsorradar.com/channels/umarashraf28)). No revenue figure exists anywhere. If the 50,000 "active" traders were paying subscribers at a blended pre-July-2026 price of $300–350 a year, annual revenue would be roughly $15–17.5M, and a conservative range allowing for marketing inflation is **$10–25M ARR — an inference, not a sourced number** — though it is consistent with "highly profitable" for a team of a few dozen and with the founder-side $100M talking point.

## Founder video, 20% creator codes and broker documentation replace paid acquisition

Fast Company's account — Ashraf "has mostly promoted the company through word of mouth and social media, including a YouTube channel with over 750,000 subscribers" — is the only near-first-party description of the go-to-market, and every observable channel is consistent with it ([Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91212604/built-by-one-of-their-own-tradezella-lets-day-traders-track-and-plan-transactions)). The founder channel is dual-purpose: aspirational titles such as "Making $18 Million Day Trading" and "How To Get RICH In Your 20s" drive reach, while journaling and "monitoring progress" recur as the bridge to the product ([Chartable: CEOCAST](https://chartable.com/podcasts/the-ceocast/episodes/142557598-umar-ashraf-28-year-old-makes-18-million-day-trading-stocks); [Chartable: First Things THRST](https://chartable.com/podcasts/first-things-thrst/episodes/157508710-e027-the-trading-expert-how-to-get-rich-in-your-20s-my-trading-method-umar-ashraf)), and the channel description reads "I use TradeZella to track and journal my trades" ([vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/UCir8ZEhgIOLY__2tN1Pi0ig)). SponsorRadar classifies TradeZella as a sponsor of Ashraf's own channel and logs **162 sponsored YouTube videos across just three channels, 155 of them on Trade Brigade (about 220K subscribers) with deals as recent as July 2026** and the 20%-off code "TB" ([SponsorRadar brand page](https://sponsorradar.com/brands/tradezella)), a narrow-and-deep footprint of always-on deals rather than a broad one. No Meta or Google advertising surfaced, and the Reddit discussion of TradeZella that was accessible concerns price rather than ad fatigue ([r/Daytrading mirror](https://lr.sudovanilla.org/r/Daytrading/comments/1ga4kgy/potential_100k_month/ltb2g1n)); the ad libraries could not be queried, so absence is not proof, but it fits a bootstrapped company that pays only for performance.

Performance marketing runs through a tiered **Partner Program**: Bronze (one paid referral) earns 20% of subscription revenue for 12 months plus a free month; Silver (10 paid customers) earns 30% for 12 months, a personalized 20%-off coupon for the partner's audience and beta access; Gold (100) and Top (400) add a 10% lifetime commission, a free lifetime account and priority support; commissions stop on cancellation, refund or chargeback, and promotion is allowed on YouTube, TikTok, X, Instagram, Discord, blogs and newsletters ([Help: Partner Program](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/12467249-tradezella-partner-program); [Partners page](https://www.tradezella.com/partners)). The Silver coupon explains the "XX20" codes across the web — WOR20 (listed as used about 6,931 times), MM20, PIPS20 "exclusive to Babypips members," TB and DGT ([Wethrift](https://www.wethrift.com/tradezella); [Modest Money](https://www.modestmoney.com/tradezella-review/); [Babypips partner page](https://partners.babypips.com/tradezella); [DamnPropFirms](https://damnpropfirms.com/?p=19038)) — and it means the de facto street price for creator-sourced customers is **20% below list**. The marketing site loads FirstPromoter's affiliate script, which identifies the tracking platform ([saved broker-support page](https://github.com/AAH20/Apex_Omega/blob/2300352a124db6df72e8ecfe664485b5213dcd0f/tz_brokersupport.html)). A credits-based user Referral Program ($50 of credit for the first successful referral, promotion to the Partner Program at five) is paused "being restructured" ([Help: Referral Program](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/12467249-tradezella-referral-program)); the company is hiring an "Affiliate Partnerships Lead" ([Careers](https://careers.tradezella.com/o/affiliate-partnerships-lead)); and a separate **Creator Program pays people with "no trading experience or followers" to post 30–60-second phone videos from TradeZella-supplied scripts, advertising $500–1,000 a month for beginners and $3,000+ for creators who regularly go viral** ([Creator Program](https://www.tradezella.com/creator-program)). The 12-month-recurring-plus-lifetime structure is sized for community owners and educators who can hit 100–400 referrals, not for one-off reviewers.

Three further layers do the work paid media would otherwise do. First, SEO: TradeZella publishes its own "TradeZella Review [2026]: Honest Assessment from Active Traders" and pricing guides to occupy branded search results ([TradeZella review post](https://www.tradezella.com/blog/tradezella-review-2026-honest); [pricing guide](https://www.tradezella.com/blog/tradezella-pricing-guide-2026)), "/vs/" pages against TraderSync, Tradervue, TradesViz, TradingView, Forex Tester and NinjaTrader ([vs TraderSync](https://www.tradezella.com/vs/tradersync)), prop-firm ranking content that captures the prop persona before they need a journal ([Best Prop Firms 2026](https://www.tradezella.com/blog/best-prop-firms-2026-rankings)), an email-gated "Free AI Prompt Pack" ([TradeZella tools](https://www.tradezella.com/tools/ai-trading-prompts)), llms.txt files for AI crawlers ([llms-full.txt](https://www.tradezella.com/llms-full.txt)), and multiple live homepage variants (home-v2 "#1 Rated Trading Journal"; the current hero "Meet Your AI Trading Partner") that indicate active landing-page testing ([home-v2](https://www.tradezella.com/home-v2); [homepage](https://tradezella.com)). Second, integrations as distribution: Interactive Brokers hosts a TradeZella page in its own third-party-integration documentation ([Interactive Brokers](https://www.interactivebrokers.com/docs/third-party-integrations/specific-third-party-connection-details/trade-zella/introduction)), TradeLocker co-announced an embedded integration on October 31, 2024 ([TradeLocker](https://tradelocker.com/newsroom/tradelocker-integration-with-tradezella/)), and the integrations index carries a "NEW: POWERED by TradeZella" banner that suggests an embed program whose terms were not retrievable ([Integrations](https://tradezella.com/integrations)). Third, community and education: a Discord joined from inside the app ([Help](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/9100649-how-to-access-tradezella-s-discord-community)) that the company sized at "10k+" on an older homepage, "20K+ active members" on its community page and "25,000+ traders" by June 6, 2026 ([home-v2](https://www.tradezella.com/home-v2); [Community](https://www.tradezella.com/community); [X](https://x.com/TradeZella/status/2063290207772840093)); "10+ events scheduled weekly," including bi-weekly Pre-Market Prep and daily 30-minute Backtesting Sessions ([Community](https://www.tradezella.com/community); [video page](https://www.tradezella.com/video-page)); free Zella University courses on trading basics, order flow and ICT ([University](https://tradezella.com/university)); and a free 30-day "Zella Zone" discipline program with Discord sessions and prizes ([Zella Zone](https://www.tradezella.com/zella-zone)).

Retention is engineered as deliberately as acquisition. A vendor case study is the clearest first-party evidence of what worked: TradeZella "was losing customers because it lacked usable user data," piped app data into BigQuery and Google Sheets to drive behaviour-triggered email sequences, and **activation rose from 28% to 65% while reactivations grew 2.5x in the first month** (vendor-reported) ([Coupler.io case study](https://www.casestudies.com/company/couplerio/case-study/how-tradezella-retained-25-times-more-customers-with-the-use-of-data)). Automated weekly and monthly performance emails go out on Mondays to anyone with at least four closed trades by Sunday ([Help: weekly and monthly reports](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/14072699-weekly-and-monthly-performance-reports-in-tradezella)); the 0–100 Zella Score, the Progress Tracker's streaks and heat map, and Spaces leaderboards turn logging into a habit loop; and shareable trade links that let recipients replay the trade, plus branded backtest-result images, give creators ready-made content ([Help: share trades](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/7898172-how-to-easily-share-your-trades-from-tradezella); [Help: share backtesting results](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/16153525-how-to-share-your-backtesting-results-in-tradezella)). Mentor Mode and Spaces are the B2B2C channel: both mentor and student must hold active subscriptions ([Help: Mentor Mode](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/8293313-understanding-mentor-mode)), so an educator who moves a community onto TradeZella forces every member to subscribe, and the Ultra tier's unlimited invites plus the Partner Program's top tiers monetize exactly that educator. Promotions are confined to annual mid-tier commitments: the Black Friday 2025 code BF25F applied only to Annual Pro and excluded existing annual plans ([Help: Black Friday guide](https://intercom.help/tradezella-4066d388d93c/en/articles/12875345-black-friday-discount-guide)), and a single Slickdeals report put the discount at 50% ([Slickdeals](https://slickdeals.net/f/18899230-tradezella-black-friday-deal-25)).

## Prices held flat from 2022 until the July 14, 2026 move to three tiers

TradeZella's list prices did not move from its 2022 launch until **July 14, 2026**. Through July 13, 2026 the lineup was Basic at **$29/month or $288/year (about $24/month)** and Premium — quietly renamed Pro during 2025–2026 — at **$49/month or $399/year (about $33.25/month)** ([StockBrokers.com](https://www.stockbrokers.com/review/tools/tradezella); [TradeZella pricing guide 2026](https://www.tradezella.com/blog/tradezella-pricing-guide-2026)); Fast Company quoted the same $24 and "from $33" annual equivalents in October 2024 ([Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91212604/built-by-one-of-their-own-tradezella-lets-day-traders-track-and-plan-transactions)). Basic covered one account and three playbooks while Pro offered unlimited playbooks, replay, full backtesting and up to 20 accounts ([TradeZella pricing guide 2026](https://www.tradezella.com/blog/tradezella-pricing-guide-2026); [DayTradingZ](https://daytradingz.com/tradezella-review/)), a cap the company's own posts had raised from 10 accounts in 2023 ([X, 2023 explainer](https://x.com/TradeZella/status/1705629792241062148)). The help center's "Our Pricing" article documents the change: Essential rose from $29 to $35 monthly and $288 to $315 annually, Pro from $49 to $59 and $399 to $531, and a new Ultra tier arrived at $99 monthly or $891 annually ([Help: Our Pricing](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/8911582-our-pricing)). The company framed it as "updating its pricing for the first time since launching in 2022, moving from two plans to three" and "a modest increase that funds continued feature development" ([TradeZella blog](https://www.tradezella.com/blog/tradezella-pricing)); PricingSaaS logged the pricing-page diff in ISO week 2026-W30 ([PricingSaaS](https://pricingsaas.com/companies/tradezella/diffs/2026W30)). Arithmetic on the cited prices shows every annual plan is exactly 25% off monthly ($315 = 0.75 × $420; $531 = 0.75 × $708; $891 = 0.75 × $1,188), replacing the old asymmetric discounts of 17% on Basic and 32% on Premium, so **Essential rose 20.7% monthly but only 9.4% annually, while Pro rose 20.4% monthly and 33.1% annually**. The Pro-annual jump, the largest single change, is what "modest" understates, and rival TradesViz publicly criticized the announcement for justifying prices "by pointing backwards at work already delivered and already paid for" ([TradesViz vs TradeZella](https://www.tradesviz.com/tradesviz-vs-tradezella/)). Grandfathering is conditional: "if you subscribed before July 14, 2026, your subscription renews at your current price. However, any plan change — upgrading, downgrading, or switching billing cycles — is charged at our current pricing" ([Help: Billing, Upgrades, Proration and Renewals](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/11794833-tradezella-billing-how-plan-upgrades-proration-and-renewals-work)), and reviewers add that cancelling and resubscribing forfeits the old rate ([PurePowerPicks](https://purepowerpicks.com/tradezella-review/)), which turns a loyalty perk into a switching cost.

## The product is an execution-level ledger wrapped in review rituals and AI

### Onboarding seeds a demo account and pushes toward import

A new account's dashboard at app.tradezella.com/tracking opens on a **"Demo account" pre-loaded with 110 trades** and a yellow banner — "You are currently viewing Demo Trades. Ready to add your own trades? [Add trades]" — so the dashboard is never empty and the primary call to action is import ([hands-on screenshot 01](notes/screenshots/01-dashboard-demo-account.png)). The demo data is itself pedagogy: a 31.78% trade win rate paired with a 3.90 average win/loss ratio ($964 versus −$247), a 1.82 profit factor, a 57.58% day win rate and $14,742 net P&L teach that a low win rate can be profitable, which is the message TradeZella's content repeats ([hands-on screenshot 01](notes/screenshots/01-dashboard-demo-account.png)). Real data enters from the Add Trade page via "Add New Account" → choose broker → file upload or sync ([Help: Account Management](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/8164981-account-management-in-tradezella)); inside the Start my day flow the import step shows three cards — Auto-sync (greyed out if the broker does not support it), File upload (labelled "Recommended") and Add manually ([UI tour](https://github.com/0xsickre/Trading_Journal/blob/eee601fbc49e4292730d5b1c77d26b4c03d7fad6/docs/tradezella-ui-tour.md)). Two generic templates exist: an execution-level `generic.csv` for stocks, options and futures with columns Date, Time, Symbol, Buy/Sell, Quantity, Price, Spread, Expiration, Strike, Call/Put, Commission, Fees, where "Spread" is actually the instrument-type marker (Stock, Single, Future, Forex, Crypto) and single-leg options carry expiry, strike and call/put in separate columns ([generic.csv copy](https://github.com/vxcall/saxo-tradezella/blob/3f7bbba2fb84d610bbf5b3c5f2da819ce19fcb21/generic.csv)), and a trade-history `generic-trade.csv` for forex and CFDs (Open Time, Close Time, Symbol, Direction, Volume, Open/Close Price, P&L, Commission, Swap, Currency) ([UI tour](https://github.com/0xsickre/Trading_Journal/blob/eee601fbc49e4292730d5b1c77d26b4c03d7fad6/docs/tradezella-ui-tour.md)). The generic importer "requires every execution individually, including both buys and sells" ([TRADE_IMPORT_BEHAVIOR citing TradeZella help](https://github.com/trading-journal-ai/trading-journal/blob/c2dea448e161ceb4ad1906f0d3baf14d5a3db019/docs/import/TRADE_IMPORT_BEHAVIOR.md); [Help: generic CSV](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/8239862-how-to-import-trades-from-unsupported-broker-into-tradezella-via-generic-csv-file-upload)).

Import friction is visible even in a controlled test. The date format is chosen separately (default MM/DD/YY) while TradeZella's own template uses YYYY-MM-DD, which made the tester's first upload fail with "No executions were able to be imported"; the import dialog has its own timezone selector, separate from the account timezone ([UI tour](https://github.com/0xsickre/Trading_Journal/blob/eee601fbc49e4292730d5b1c77d26b4c03d7fad6/docs/tradezella-ui-tour.md)); and chart markers that look wrong are "often caused by trades being imported with the wrong timezone selected," with the documented fix being to delete the import and re-upload ([Help: correcting chart entry and exit points](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/6153644-correcting-chart-entry-and-exit-points-in-tradezella)). Imports are tracked as batches: Settings → Import History records each attempt with account, broker, upload date, transaction count, trade count and status, and the trash icon undoes an import, "permanently" removing its trades, executions and related journal entries ([Help: Import History](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/6706087-import-history-on-tradezella); [Help: Undo imports](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/7898222-how-to-undo-imports-in-tradezella)). A one-time **First Import Analysis** is generated on the first file upload, lives under Reports → Recaps & Insights and ends with an opt-in to weekly and monthly report emails ([Help: First Import Analysis](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/14072606-first-import-analysis-in-tradezella)). Account-level configuration lives in Settings: commission and fee rules are applied only to imported trades that lack fees ([Help: commissions and fees](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/6954370-how-to-set-default-commissions-and-fees-for-your-trades)); default profit-target and stop-loss rules can be scoped to all trades or a symbol or instrument, with a checkbox that decides whether they overwrite existing values ([Help: default PT/SL](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/12053164-setting-default-profit-target-and-stop-loss-for-your-trades)); and the display currency applies to all accounts and is limited to **USD, EUR, AUD and GBP** ([Help: currency](https://intercom.help/tradezella-4066d388d93c/en/articles/8011851-how-to-change-your-account-currency-in-tradezella)).

### The dashboard and Zella Score turn fills into a 0–100 habit score

The dashboard chrome is a dark-purple top bar with the wordmark, a prominent "Zella AI" button, notifications and profile; a collapsed left rail of icons (add trades, dashboard, calendar, notebook, reports, strategies, prop firm, then replay, backtesting and a folder); and right-aligned controls for display unit ("Dollar"), Filters, Date range and account selector ([hands-on screenshot 01](notes/screenshots/01-dashboard-demo-account.png)). The help center describes two sections — an upper strip for key metrics limited to five widgets (Net P&L, Trade Win %, Profit Factor, Day Win %, Trade Expectancy, streaks, Max and Average Drawdown) and a lower area for visual insights including the Zella Score and "Recent Trades & Open Positions" — plus a Template button that edits or creates saved widget layouts and a May 2024 "Chart Library" ([Help: dashboard widgets](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/7118437-understanding-dashboard-widgets-and-stats); [Help: add/edit widgets](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/9718410-how-to-add-edit-dashboard-widgets); [May 2024 updates](https://www.tradezella.com/blog/whats-new-may-2024)); the August 2026 tester reported the layout as fixed, a conflict most likely explained by the tester not opening the Template control ([UI tour](https://github.com/0xsickre/Trading_Journal/blob/eee601fbc49e4292730d5b1c77d26b4c03d7fad6/docs/tradezella-ui-tour.md)). The populated default shows Net P&L, a Trade win % gauge with win/breakeven/loss counts, a Profit factor donut, a Day win % gauge, an Avg win/loss bar, the Zella score radar, a Progress tracker heat map, Daily net cumulative P&L, Net daily P&L bars, Recent trades/Open positions, Account balance with a deposits/withdrawals series, an inverted Drawdown area and a monthly calendar ([hands-on screenshot 01](notes/screenshots/01-dashboard-demo-account.png); [UI tour](https://github.com/0xsickre/Trading_Journal/blob/eee601fbc49e4292730d5b1c77d26b4c03d7fad6/docs/tradezella-ui-tour.md)). Seven unit views switch the whole dashboard: Dollars (default), Percentage (requires an initial balance; daily figures relative to that day's opening balance), Privacy (hides balances and P&L), R-Multiple (only trades with entered initial risk), Ticks and Points (futures) and Pips (forex) ([Help: Dashboard Views](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/9858998-dashboard-views-in-tradezella)).

The metric definitions are the most reusable part of the documentation. **Net P&L is based on closed trades only**; floating P&L is not shown for imported trades and "isn't included in any statistics," and there is no account-wide floating drawdown — a Drawdown widget tracks day-level drawdown while per-trade running P&L lives on the trade page ([Help: Unrealized P&L FAQ](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/12572470-unrealized-p-l-faq)). Day Win % is winning trading days divided by all trading days; Current Streak combines winning-trade and winning-day streaks ([Help: dashboard widgets](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/7118437-understanding-dashboard-widgets-and-stats)). Drawdown is computed from cumulative P&L in the dollar view and from the running balance including deposits and withdrawals in the percentage view; Max Drawdown is the deepest single decline while the Drawdown widget tracks cumulative declines ([Help: drawdown widgets](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/8427754-understanding-drawdown-widgets-in-tradezella)). The calendar **books P&L on the day a trade is closed or partially exited**, so a trade opened August 10 and closed August 12 shows on the 12th, and a partial on the 14th shows that portion on the 14th ([Help: calendar P&L](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/10528734-how-the-dashboard-calendar-calculates-and-shows-daily-profit-loss-p-l)); the July 2024 Advanced Calendar adds weekly running totals, days traded per week, a note icon on journaled days and user-chosen per-day stats (R-Multiple, Day P&L, Ticks, number of trades, Day Win %) ([Help: advanced calendar](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/9689020-advanced-calendar-widget-in-tradezella-dashboard)).

The **Zella Score**, relaunched on December 17, 2024 as a six-metric composite replacing a three-metric score ([X](https://x.com/TradeZella/status/1867685776449814855); [December 2024 updates](https://www.tradezella.com/blog/whats-new-december-2024)), is weighted **Profit Factor 25%, Average Win/Loss 20%, Max Drawdown 20%, Trade Win % 15%, Recovery Factor 10% and Consistency 10%**, each sub-score on 0–100 ([Help: Zella Score](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/10305642-introducing-the-all-new-zella-score)). The published scoring rules mix two normalizations: win rate is linear against a "Top Threshold" of 60 (a 25% win rate scores 25 ÷ 60 × 100 = 41.67, and anything above 60% caps at 100); average win/loss uses lookup bands (2.6 or more scores 100, 2.4–2.59 scores 90–99, down to below 1.8 scoring 20); max drawdown scores 100 minus the peak-to-trough drop as a percentage of the maximum cumulative P&L before the drop; recovery factor (net profit over max drawdown) scores 100 at 3.5 and 60–69 at 2.5–2.99; and the consistency rule is only partly visible ("if average profit < 0, then 0") ([Help: Zella Score](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/10305642-introducing-the-all-new-zella-score)). The profit-factor bands were not retrievable. The score renders as a hexagonal radar with a 0–100 number on a red-to-green gradient, and the predecessor metric (the 2022 "Zella Scale") has since been reused as the name of a per-trade MAE/MFE bar, so the same word has meant two things in the product's history ([UI tour](https://github.com/0xsickre/Trading_Journal/blob/eee601fbc49e4292730d5b1c77d26b4c03d7fad6/docs/tradezella-ui-tour.md)).

### Trade View and the trade page hold the review workflow

Trade View (help center: the "All tab" or "Trade Log page") is a paginated table of 50 trades per page with ten default columns — Open date, Symbol, Status, Close date, Volume, Net P&L, Net ROI, Zella Insights, Zella Scale, Reviewed — and roughly 43 selectable ones, including Best exit (%, P&L, price, time), Position and Price MAE/MFE, Initial risk, Initial target, Realized R Multiple, Pips, Return per pip, Ticks per contract, Total swap and Trade rating ([Help: All tab](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/7143872-understanding-the-all-tab); [UI tour](https://github.com/0xsickre/Trading_Journal/blob/eee601fbc49e4292730d5b1c77d26b4c03d7fad6/docs/tradezella-ui-tour.md)). Filters come in five groups — General (instrument, intraday/multiday, open/closed, reviewed, side, symbol, status, rating, prices, R-multiple, position size), Tags (Setups, Mistakes, Custom), Day & Time, Strategy (with "Excluding," "match all" and "None") and Zella Insights (beta) ([Help: Using Filters](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/12417670-using-filters-in-tradezella)). Bulk actions cover mark as reviewed or not reviewed, CSV export of active or all columns (which cannot be re-imported), merge and split, delete (notes and tags go too, with notes recoverable from Trash for 30 days) and add to a strategy ([Help: All tab](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/7143872-understanding-the-all-tab); [Help: export](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/9725069-how-to-export-data-to-a-csv-file-from-the-trade-log-page); [Help: delete](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/5858166-how-can-i-delete-a-trade)).

The trade page is two columns with independent tab groups — left Stats · Strategy · Executions · Attachments, right Chart · Notes · Running P&L — under a header with previous/next navigation, "Mark as reviewed," "Replay," "Share" and a gear that holds chart settings and "Delete trade" ([Help: trade detail page](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/5860216-understanding-the-trade-detail-page); [UI tour](https://github.com/0xsickre/Trading_Journal/blob/eee601fbc49e4292730d5b1c77d26b4c03d7fad6/docs/tradezella-ui-tour.md)). There is no separate edit form: **adding and editing a trade are the same mechanism, editing the ordered execution list** (Date & Time, Instrument, Quantity, Multiplier, Price, Swap, Fee, Commission, running Position, Adjusted Cost, Adjusted Proceed, Gross P&L), rows are inline-editable, a new row defaults to the opposite side, and Symbol and Type are immutable after creation ([UI tour](https://github.com/0xsickre/Trading_Journal/blob/eee601fbc49e4292730d5b1c77d26b4c03d7fad6/docs/tradezella-ui-tour.md)). The Stats tab exposes Net P&L, Side, Account, Commissions & Fees, Total Swap, Net ROI, Gross P&L, Adjusted Cost, Strategy, Zella Scale, Price MAE/MFE, a Running P&L sparkline, **Trade Rating in half-star steps from 0.5 to 5**, Profit Target, Stop loss, Initial Target, Trade Risk, Planned and Realized R-Multiple, average entry/exit and times, and three draggable tag categories (Setups, Mistakes, Custom Tags) with "+ Add new category" and "Manage tags" ([UI tour](https://github.com/0xsickre/Trading_Journal/blob/eee601fbc49e4292730d5b1c77d26b4c03d7fad6/docs/tradezella-ui-tour.md)). The Getting Started guide prescribes the per-trade routine — enter the R-multiple and tags (especially Mistake tags), rate the trade, mark it Reviewed ([Help: Getting started](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/13863136-getting-started-with-tradezella)) — which makes "reviewed" a manual boolean decoupled from whether a note or rating exists.

Risk fields are entered after the fact. Trade Risk is the dollar amount initially risked, derived from the stop loss entered on the trade page; Initial Target is derived from the entered profit target; Planned R-Multiple is Initial Target ÷ Trade Risk ("aim for 2 or more, though anything above 1R is acceptable"); Realized R-Multiple is Net P&L ÷ Trade Risk; and for options the stop and target must be set on the option's price, not the underlying ([Help: R-Multiple](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/7190710-understanding-r-multiple)). Since **February 3, 2026** trades accept multiple profit targets and stop losses with default rules and six calculation types — Price, Price Movement, Ticks, Points, P&L and Pips — and the advanced mode computes planned profit and risk across all exit levels ([Canny, Feb 3, 2026](https://tradezella.canny.io/changelog/weekly-tradezella-updates-february-3-2026); [Help: PT/SL on the trade page](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/12450459-how-to-set-profit-targets-and-stop-losses-in-tradezella-trade-page)). The derived excursion metrics are computed from **TradeZella's own price feed, not the user's prices**: when the tester imported a fictitious EURUSD entry at 1.087 while the market was near 1.168, the product still produced real-market Price MAE/MFE of 1.16866/1.17003 and a Running P&L curve that started at an absurd ~$4,981 and ended at the realized $360, while a manually entered trade showed "Calculating…" ([UI tour](https://github.com/0xsickre/Trading_Journal/blob/eee601fbc49e4292730d5b1c77d26b4c03d7fad6/docs/tradezella-ui-tour.md)). The per-trade Zella Scale is a dollar bar from Maximum Loss (MAE) to Maximum Profit (MFE) with the realized result marked, "Best exit" columns express the MFE as a price, time, P&L and percentage, and the Chart tab embeds a full TradingView chart with drawing tools, indicators, "Show events" entry/exit markers and drawings that autosave since October 20, 2026 ([UI tour](https://github.com/0xsickre/Trading_Journal/blob/eee601fbc49e4292730d5b1c77d26b4c03d7fad6/docs/tradezella-ui-tour.md); [Canny](https://tradezella.canny.io/changelog)). **Zella Insights** adds AI pattern flags at trade and day level — holding a trade "longer than 75% of your winning trades," a "revenge cycle day," or "you had 6 trades today and you usually average 2 trades when you have a Green Day" — but the help article still describes it as early access by request, and the tester never saw the column populated ([Help: Zella Insights](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/12418995-zella-insights); [UI tour](https://github.com/0xsickre/Trading_Journal/blob/eee601fbc49e4292730d5b1c77d26b4c03d7fad6/docs/tradezella-ui-tour.md)). Asset classes are Stock, Option, Future, Future Option, Forex, Crypto and CFD with execution columns that change by type; futures and options need a contract multiplier, expiration and strike, and a wrong futures expiration "can stop chart data from loading" ([Help: add a futures trade manually](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/6492402-how-to-add-a-futures-trade-manually-in-tradezella)).

### Journaling runs through one Notebook and a Start my day ritual

Journaling is a single Notebook with system folders — All notes, Favorites, Trade Notes, Daily Journal, Sessions Recap, My notes — plus custom folders and a Trash with 30-day recovery; the Daily Journal, Trade Notes and Sessions Recap folders cannot be deleted, and each folder can carry a default template that auto-populates new notes ([Help: organizing notes](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/7190696-organizing-and-managing-your-notes-within-your-tradezella-notebook); [Help: default template](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/6191674-how-to-create-a-default-template-for-adding-notes)). The template picker ships **13 built-ins** — Daily Game Plan, All-in-One/Daily, Intra-day Check-in, Pre-Market Prep, Quarterly Roadmap, Strengths & Weaknesses, Becoming Aware of Emotions, Weekly Recap, two Trade Recap variants and Daily, Weekly and Monthly Report Cards — grouped under Favourites, Recommended and My templates ([UI tour](https://github.com/0xsickre/Trading_Journal/blob/eee601fbc49e4292730d5b1c77d26b4c03d7fad6/docs/tradezella-ui-tour.md)). A Daily Journal note is one object per trading day, shared across every trade of that day, reachable from the Day tab and from the trade page's Notes tab (sub-tabs "Trade note" and "Daily Journal"), and it auto-embeds a day card with Net P&L and trade count ([Help: Trade Replay 2.0](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/7898284-trade-replay-2-0-a-powerful-tool-for-analyzing-trades-second-by-second); [UI tour](https://github.com/0xsickre/Trading_Journal/blob/eee601fbc49e4292730d5b1c77d26b4c03d7fad6/docs/tradezella-ui-tour.md)); a trade note can be written before the trade is imported and linked afterwards ([Help](https://intercom.help/tradezella-4066d388d93c/en/articles/11517879-create-a-trade-note-before-importing-a-trade-and-link-it-later)). A new text editor with image support and PDF export shipped on August 27, 2025 ([Help: new text editor](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/8410320-introducing-our-new-and-updated-text-editor)).

The daily ritual is **Start my day**: Planning (an optional AI "Market Sentiment Briefing" that shows its price up front at "~11.29 credits/run," notes from a template, an economic-event calendar) → Trading (a live stopwatch, notes carried over from the plan, a screenshot drop zone for PNG/JPG up to 10 MB) → Import Trades → Trade Review (the day's net P&L, win rate and trade count, a trade-by-trade Running P&L chart, a trades table with WIN/LOSS/BREAKEVEN badges, the day's rules pulled from the Progress Tracker with ✓/✗, and a reflection note), closed by **Finish My Day**, which "locks in the day's progress and counts it toward your streak and heat map" ([UI tour](https://github.com/0xsickre/Trading_Journal/blob/eee601fbc49e4292730d5b1c77d26b4c03d7fad6/docs/tradezella-ui-tour.md); [Help: Progress Tracker](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/10352042-getting-started-with-the-progress-tracker)). The flow can be run retroactively for any past day, mood is logged on a five-point scale from very negative to very positive but only for days started through the ritual ([Help: Day tab](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/5829446-understanding-the-day-tab)), a "Rate My Day" feature followed on August 25, 2026, a simplified redesign entered beta on September 1, 2026 and gained its own Finish My Day button on October 20, 2026 ([Canny](https://tradezella.canny.io/changelog); [Canny, Sep 1, 2026](https://tradezella.canny.io/changelog/weekly-tradezella-updates-september-1-2026)). The data model implied is three note layers per day (plan, in-session, reflection) plus per-trade notes, all surfaced through one Daily Journal object, with "Sessions Recap" as a fourth, date-range-scoped note type that matches the marketed "Session Recaps" for weekly, monthly or quarterly reviews.

### Reports are one parameterised template multiplied by dimensions

Reports (`/tracking/reports`) has tabs Performance (new; Summary, Days, Trades), Overview, a Reports dropdown, Compare and Calendar; the dropdown holds seven pages — Day & Time (days, months, entry/exit time at 5-, 15-, 30- or 60-minute buckets, duration buckets from under a minute to 4h+), Symbols (symbols, instruments, entry-price ranges), Risk (volumes, position sizes, R-multiples), Strategies (with a "None" bucket), Tags (one tab per tag category), Options: Days till expiration, and Wins vs Losses ([UI tour](https://github.com/0xsickre/Trading_Journal/blob/eee601fbc49e4292730d5b1c77d26b4c03d7fad6/docs/tradezella-ui-tour.md); [Help: Reports Day & Time](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/11391581-reports-day-time); [Help: Reports Tags](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/11595729-reports-tags)). Every dimensional report follows one template: four highlight cards (best, least, most active, best win rate), a dual-axis chart of Net P&L and trade count, win-percentage bars, a summary table with a column picker, and a **"Cross analysis" heat map** that crosses the primary dimension with a secondary filter — account, playbook, tag, symbol or time — with a metric toggle (win rate, P&L, trades) and a Top-N limiter ([UI tour](https://github.com/0xsickre/Trading_Journal/blob/eee601fbc49e4292730d5b1c77d26b4c03d7fad6/docs/tradezella-ui-tour.md); [Help: Reports Day & Time](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/11391581-reports-day-time)). Compare "generates reports comparing two sets of trades depending on the filters you've selected" (symbols, tags, side, dates, P&L), returning full statistics, an evaluation donut and a cumulative curve per group ([Help: Compare](https://intercom.help/tradezella-4066d388d93c/en/articles/7180119-reports-compare-section)). Overview is a dense list of roughly 40 statistics from total P&L and average hold time (all, winning, losing, scratch) through max consecutive wins and losses, logged days, average planned and realized R-multiple, trade expectancy and max and average drawdown ([UI tour](https://github.com/0xsickre/Trading_Journal/blob/eee601fbc49e4292730d5b1c77d26b4c03d7fad6/docs/tradezella-ui-tour.md)). "Reports 2.0" was roadmapped on November 29, 2024 and went live in May 2025 alongside monthly recap emails ([Upvoty](https://trade-zella.upvoty.com/b/improvements/reports-20-launch/); [May 2025 updates](https://www.tradezella.com/blog/may-2025-updates)). The marketed "300+ analytics reports across 7 dashboard views" is combinatorial — seven report pages times their sub-tabs times cross-analysis dimensions times seven unit views — not 300 screens ([TradeZella blog](https://www.tradezella.com/blog/tradezella-pricing)); the only export is the Trade View CSV, with no PDF or full backup ([Help: export](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/9725069-how-to-export-data-to-a-csv-file-from-the-trade-log-page)).

### Strategies and the Progress Tracker encode rules and adherence

"Playbooks" were renamed **Strategies**, though the help article kept its ID and the in-app link's aria-label still reads "Playbooks/Strategies" ([Help: Getting Started with Strategies](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/7020769-getting-started-with-strategies); [UI tour](https://github.com/0xsickre/Trading_Journal/blob/eee601fbc49e4292730d5b1c77d26b4c03d7fad6/docs/tradezella-ui-tour.md)). A Strategy is a named, iconized setup made of free-named rule groups ("E.g. Entry criteria") whose rules can be shown Always or only for Winner, Loser or Break-even trades; after linking trades the user ticks the rules followed, and each rule reports **Follow rate, Net P&L, Profit factor and Win rate**, while each strategy reports Expectancy, Win Rate and Profit Factor ([Help: Getting Started with Strategies](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/7020769-getting-started-with-strategies); [UI tour](https://github.com/0xsickre/Trading_Journal/blob/eee601fbc49e4292730d5b1c77d26b4c03d7fad6/docs/tradezella-ui-tour.md)). Missed trades can be logged by symbol and hypothetical time, the Trades tab toggles Executed, Missed and Sample, and once a trade is linked to a rule that rule's "show when" setting is locked ([Help: Getting Started with Strategies](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/7020769-getting-started-with-strategies); [Help: Getting started with the playbook](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/7020769-getting-started-with-the-playbook)). Caps rose from 3 playbooks on Basic to **10 strategies on Essential** (observed as "My Strategies (2/10)") with Pro and Ultra unlimited ([Help: Our Pricing](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/8911582-our-pricing); [UI tour](https://github.com/0xsickre/Trading_Journal/blob/eee601fbc49e4292730d5b1c77d26b4c03d7fad6/docs/tradezella-ui-tour.md)). The "Create a new strategy" screen defaults to a template gallery filterable by asset class with 16+ playbooks by named educators (ICT models, Break & Retest, Liquidity Playbook, Market Auction Theory, Mean Reversion and more) offering "Add to my strategies," "Preview" and "Backtest this template," mirrored by a public gallery at tradezella.com/strategies ([UI tour](https://github.com/0xsickre/Trading_Journal/blob/eee601fbc49e4292730d5b1c77d26b4c03d7fad6/docs/tradezella-ui-tour.md); [Strategies gallery](https://www.tradezella.com/strategies)); "Playbook 2.0: Download pre-made playbooks" was announced April 5, 2025 and shipped as templates in May 2025 ([X](https://x.com/TradeZella/status/1908526063249940667); [May 2025 updates](https://www.tradezella.com/blog/may-2025-updates)). No revenue share for template authors was found; the gallery is a distribution channel for educators, not a marketplace.

The **Progress Tracker** (beta February 27, 2025; public launch April 23, 2025) scores each selected trading day against rules grouped into PREPARE, TRADE and REFLECT stages ([Upvoty changelog](https://trade-zella.upvoty.com/f/changelog/); [X](https://x.com/TradeZella/status/1915073327997112707)). Defaults observed are "Start my day by [09:30]" (Prepare), "Trading hours" from/to in the account timezone (Trade), and "Link trades to playbook," "Input Stop loss to all trades," "Net max loss /trade" with a $ or % toggle and "Net max loss /day" (Reflect), plus free-form custom rules with day ranges ([UI tour](https://github.com/0xsickre/Trading_Journal/blob/eee601fbc49e4292730d5b1c77d26b4c03d7fad6/docs/tradezella-ui-tour.md)). Rules apply only on selected trading days, mandatory rules must be satisfied before finishing the day, incomplete rules "remain unchecked, and you won't be able to modify them after finishing your day," edits "only update your scoring for today and for future days," an optional email reminder warns before a streak is lost, and idle days no longer reset the streak ([Help: Progress Tracker rules](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/10371695-understanding-and-using-progress-tracker-rules); [Help: Getting Started with the Progress Tracker](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/10352042-getting-started-with-the-progress-tracker); [Upvoty changelog](https://trade-zella.upvoty.com/f/changelog/)). The screen shows today's progress, current streak, a mood emoji, a GitHub-style heat map and a rules table with RULE, CONDITION, RULE STREAK, AVERAGE PERFORMANCE and FOLLOW RATE columns, which implies per-rule daily results are persisted; "Reset all progress" deletes streaks, rules and achievements across all accounts, so rules are user-level rather than account-level ([UI tour](https://github.com/0xsickre/Trading_Journal/blob/eee601fbc49e4292730d5b1c77d26b4c03d7fad6/docs/tradezella-ui-tour.md); [Help: reset](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/12141413-how-to-reset-your-progress-tracker)). The evaluation model mixes time-based rules checked against timestamps, data rules auto-evaluated from trade records, and named custom rules that are manual check-offs. No separate goals or targets feature was found.

### Backtesting and Trade Replay run on TradeZella's own price history

Backtesting launched in January 2024 as "one of our biggest product updates" ([January 2024 release notes](https://www.tradezella.com/blog/january-2024-release-notes)). A manual session takes up to **five symbols**, a date range and a starting balance, on data that "goes back to 2014 depending on the symbol" ([Help: create a backtesting session](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/8835814-how-to-create-a-backtesting-session)); the order panel calculates contracts or lots from the user's max risk, the stop can be static or trailing, stops and targets are draggable on the chart, and the Long/Short position tool syncs with the order panel ([Help: backtesting window](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/8866881-understanding-the-backtesting-window)). Playback can never run faster than the chart timeframe, multi-chart layouts sync to the lowest timeframe, and **seconds timeframes are Pro and Ultra only** ([Help: timeframes and playback](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/8800462-what-timeframes-and-playback-speeds-can-i-use-in-backtesting)); a "Smooth Candles" toggle fills gaps in chart data, which tells you the data has gaps ([Help: Smooth Candles](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/10210901-smooth-candles-feature-in-backtesting)). Every simulated trade is auto-logged into a dedicated Backtesting area with its own Trade View, Notebook, Reports and Strategies, where it can be tagged, noted and linked to a strategy ([Help: backtested trades journaled](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/8800482-do-backtested-trades-get-automatically-journaled-in-tradezella)); blog posts that say backtests flow into "the same journal" are marketing simplifications, and the help center's separate-area description is authoritative. Marketing dates the data to September 2014 across forex, futures, stocks and crypto ([llms-full.txt](https://www.tradezella.com/llms-full.txt); [Backtesting page](https://www.tradezella.com/backtesting)); the vendor is undisclosed.

Two AI layers were added in 2026. **Guided Backtesting** (September 1, 2026) puts Zella AI inside a live session, where it can read the chart, control the replay, add indicators and compare the user's execution to a strategy's sample trades, but cannot change settings, balances, commissions, subscriptions or broker connections; seeking backward prompts a confirmation because later orders are removed ([Help: Zella AI in a backtesting session](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/16745671-how-to-use-zella-ai-in-a-backtesting-session); [Canny, Sep 1, 2026](https://tradezella.canny.io/changelog/weekly-tradezella-updates-september-1-2026)). **Automated Backtesting** is a different engine: the user sets Symbol, Session, Period and Trading Days and describes entry rules, exit rules, stop and target in plain English; Zella AI "turns the description into structured entry rules, exit rules, and trade controls," asks clarifying questions, and runs over full history, but only within a whitelist of supported concepts (fair value gaps, order blocks, liquidity sweeps, change of character, SMT divergence, EMA crossovers, session filters) — "you can't add custom indicators that TradeZella doesn't support" ([Help: Automated Backtesting](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/15934576-how-to-use-automated-backtesting-in-tradezella); [Help: describing your strategy](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/17570099-how-to-describe-your-strategy-for-automated-backtesting-supported-concepts-entry-rules)). It is gated by runs rather than credits: **Pro 10 and Ultra 100 completed runs per month, Essential one free run**, with re-runs of modified sessions not counted ([Help: Automated Backtesting](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/15934576-how-to-use-automated-backtesting-in-tradezella); [TradeZella blog](https://www.tradezella.com/blog/tradezella-pricing)). The results page opens with "a one-line verdict on whether the numbers look trustworthy yet," then headline stats, the equity curve, the parsed strategy and a Zella AI analysis, with the caveat that results are "the simulated version, not the exact strategy you wrote" ([Help: reading results](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/16140728-how-to-read-your-automated-backtesting-results-in-tradezella)). The weekly changelog shows the rule engine being extended feature by feature through autumn 2026 — trailing stops and partial exits, ATR and EMA trailing, DXY, FTSE and JP225, volume-profile levels (September 29), Fibonacci entries (October 6, "the most requested strategy feature"), a redesigned results page (October 13) and, on October 20, failed runs halved to a **95.7% completion rate** ([Canny](https://tradezella.canny.io/changelog)), which is how you can tell it is a hand-built rule vocabulary rather than arbitrary code generation. TradeZella's own guidance is that "below 30 trades, the results are essentially random" ([TradeZella blog](https://www.tradezella.com/blog/what-is-automated-backtesting)); Trustpilot reviewers call the backtester both "buggy" and "lacks indicators" and "better than alternatives" ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/tradezella.com)).

**Trade Replay** replays the user's own executions, "execution by execution, from entry to exit, using real market price movement," with green and red dots on a timeline for buys and sells, a step interval of 1 second to 1 minute, a speed control, an optional second chart, multi-trade sessions and a Day Replay of a whole session ([Help: Trade Replay 2.0](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/7898284-trade-replay-2-0-a-powerful-tool-for-analyzing-trades-second-by-second); [Help: jump to executions](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/6702951-how-to-jump-to-specific-executions-in-the-replay-feature); [Help: Day tab](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/5829446-understanding-the-day-tab)). The two "2.0" help articles carry 2023-era Intercom IDs but December 18, 2025 dates, so the December 2025 "Replay 2.0" is a relaunch of a mid-2023 feature; the help center distinguishes it cleanly from backtesting — "Trade Replay works on your past executions, while Backtesting tests a strategy on historical data you haven't traded yet" ([Help: replay vs backtesting](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/11787298-what-is-the-trade-replay-feature-in-tradezella-and-how-does-it-compare-to-backtesting)). Essential gets 10 replays a month without the 1-second chart, Pro and Ultra unlimited replays with 1-second data and "Trade Replay AI Insights" ([Help: Our Pricing](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/8911582-our-pricing)), and the feature is blocked on mobile user agents (see below). Trustpilot reviewers call replay "essential" and "stronger than FXReplay," and a third-party comparison calls it TradeZella's "signature feature" ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/tradezella.com); [TraderNotion](https://www.tradernotion.com/blog/tradezella-vs-edgewonk-2026)).

### Zella AI meters chat, agents and an MCP server in credits

The Zella AI name first appeared in an April 5, 2025 roadmap post ([X](https://x.com/TradeZella/status/1908526063249940667)); a help article documents a chat assistant grounded only in the account's own data with 500 and 1,000 monthly credits on the old Basic and Premium plans ([Help: What Is Zella AI](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/11201153-what-is-zella-ai-tradezella-s-ai-trading-assistant)); and the May 26–29, 2026 relaunch repositioned it as "the first AI trading partner built for all active traders," one that "will not give buy or sell picks, and makes no predictions" because "every answer comes from your data" ([TradeZella blog](https://www.tradezella.com/blog/zella-ai-your-ai-trading-partner); [X](https://x.com/TradeZella/status/2060452738694295705)). The assistant "only accesses the trading data already in your TradeZella account," "does not share your data externally," keeps a memory of "your style, your goals, your recurring mistakes," does not place trades, opens from anywhere with ⌘+Z then Enter and picks up context from the trade page, Notes, Reports, Dashboard, Day view, Backtesting or Strategies ([Help: What Is Zella AI](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/11201153-what-is-zella-ai-tradezella-s-ai-trading-assistant); [Help: Zella AI in a backtesting session](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/16745671-how-to-use-zella-ai-in-a-backtesting-session)); the purple chat bubble in the bottom-right corner is the Intercom support assistant, not Zella AI ([Help: Getting started](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/13863136-getting-started-with-tradezella)). On **July 22, 2026** three agents went live — a pre-market plan before the open (Market Sentiment Briefing), an Auto Trade Tagger that applies user-written rules such as "tag as revenge if I took a trade within 5 minutes of a losing trade" to every fill, and a Session Review that "automatically compares your morning plan against your actual results" — gated to Pro and Ultra ([X](https://x.com/TradeZella/status/2079960500016914928); [TradeZella blog: AI agents](https://www.tradezella.com/blog/ai-trading-agents); [Zella AI page](https://www.tradezella.com/zella-ai); [TradeZella blog](https://www.tradezella.com/blog/tradezella-pricing)). Since September 29, 2026 the assistant also answers product questions from the help-center knowledge base ([Canny](https://tradezella.canny.io/changelog)).

Metering is explicit. "Credits fuel everything Zella AI does — chatting about your performance, session reviews, auto-tagging, and reports," each message costs "a small number of credits based on the complexity of your request," balances reset monthly and cannot be topped up, and usage is visible under Settings → Subscription → Usage Details ([pricing page](https://www.tradezella.com/pricing); [Help: What Is Zella AI](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/11201153-what-is-zella-ai-tradezella-s-ai-trading-assistant)). The only observed price is the briefing's "~11.29 credits/run" ([UI tour](https://github.com/0xsickre/Trading_Journal/blob/eee601fbc49e4292730d5b1c77d26b4c03d7fad6/docs/tradezella-ui-tour.md)), and one reviewer "burned through 25 credits" in an hour of chat ([StockBrokers.com](https://www.stockbrokers.com/review/tools/tradezella)), which puts ordinary messages at roughly one to three credits and Essential's 500 at a few hundred turns or about 40 briefings a month. Reception splits: the same reviewer found Zella AI "a pleasure to use" and said it "pointed out where I went astray"; a Trustpilot reviewer judged it "more mature than Claude and Grok for trade review" ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/tradezella.com)); a two-star reviewer quoted by a comparison site said it "corrupted about 200 of their trades and made their statistics unreliable" ([Deepvue](https://deepvue.com/compare/tradezella-alternatives/)); and a third-party review summarizes the objection as "the 2026 pitch is the AI, and the AI is metered" ([Quantum-Algo](https://www.quantum-algo.com/blog/guides/tradezella-review/)). The **MCP server** at `https://mcp.tradezella.com/mcp` went into beta on October 6, 2026 and was submitted to the Claude and OpenAI connector directories on October 20 ([Canny](https://tradezella.canny.io/changelog)); a user adds it as a custom connector in Claude, signs in through a TradeZella page with "no passwords or API keys," and chooses "Read only (selected by default) or Read & write," where read-write extends only to notes, tags and strategies and "in both modes, Claude cannot add, edit, or delete individual trades" ([Help: Connect Claude to TradeZella](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/17441265-how-to-connect-claude-to-tradezella-mcp)); the terminal path is `claude mcp add --transport http tradezella https://mcp.tradezella.com/mcp` ([Help: Connect Claude Code](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/17441332-how-to-connect-claude-code-terminal-to-tradezella-mcp)). The consent design — read-only by default, writes limited to annotations, execution data immutable to external agents — reads as a direct response to the data-corruption complaint, and it is the single most transferable AI design decision in the product.

### Mentor Mode, Spaces and PropFirm Sync extend the journal to coaches and prop traders

**Mentor Mode**, announced by the founder on August 29, 2023 and live the following month, lets a mentor invite students by email or link, see up to five students' live P&L and stats on one dashboard, and open any consenting student's Dashboard, Trade Log, Notebook, Playbooks and Reports in view-only mode while leaving comments on journal entries; students choose which accounts to share and can revoke access in one click, and "both the mentor and mentee must have an active TradeZella subscription" ([X](https://x.com/UmarAshraf/status/1696579167289053575); [Help: Understanding Mentor Mode](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/8293313-understanding-mentor-mode); [Mentor Mode page](https://www.tradezella.com/mentor-mode)). The July 2026 matrix gates it as Join (Essential), Invite + Join (Pro) and Unlimited (Ultra), the nav still labels it BETA, and a September 15, 2026 fix addressed "mentors could accept their own invites" ([TradeZella blog](https://www.tradezella.com/blog/tradezella-pricing); [UI tour](https://github.com/0xsickre/Trading_Journal/blob/eee601fbc49e4292730d5b1c77d26b4c03d7fad6/docs/tradezella-ui-tour.md); [Canny](https://tradezella.canny.io/changelog)). **Spaces**, promoted on June 6, 2026 as "mentor-led communities with feed, chat, classroom, leaderboard," generalizes the same permission primitive to groups: a member connects an account and "their live data flows in automatically," leaderboards rank P&L and win rate by period from "every member's live journal," rooms are private or public and created "in 60 seconds," mentors publish structured courses, and the feature was "invite-only at launch" ([X](https://x.com/TradeZella/status/2063290207772840093); [Spaces page](https://www.tradezella.com/spaces)); tiers are Join, Join + Create and Unlimited ([TradeZella blog](https://www.tradezella.com/blog/tradezella-pricing)). No separate price exists for either, and no public trader profiles exist outside Space leaderboards.

**PropFirm Sync** is "a free feature built into TradeZella that centralizes your entire prop firm operation" across "200+ prop firms": every evaluation, funded and breached account with its size, rules, phase, status and step count; spend on evaluations and resets; payouts (Wise and WorkMarket supported); net ROI; rule compliance and drawdown progress; and firm comparisons ([Help: What Is PropFirm Sync](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/14499729-what-is-propfirm-sync-tradezella-s-command-center-for-prop-firm-traders)). Finances are entered manually or detected automatically by connecting a bank or card through **Plaid**, which currently recognises ten futures firms — TopStep, Apex Trader Funding, Tradeify, Take Profit Trader, Bulenox, Top One Futures, My Funded Futures, Funded Next, Alpha Futures and Lucid Trading — and each tracked account step can spawn or attach a journal account ([Help: What Is PropFirm Sync](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/14499729-what-is-propfirm-sync-tradezella-s-command-center-for-prop-firm-traders); [Help: Getting Started with PropFirm Sync](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/13463710-getting-started-with-the-propfirm-sync-feature)). It shipped as "Profirm Sync (beta)" in the April 7, 2026 changelog and was a top-level nav item by August 2026 ([Canny, Apr 7, 2026](https://tradezella.canny.io/changelog/weekly-tradezella-updates-april-7-2026); [UI tour](https://github.com/0xsickre/Trading_Journal/blob/eee601fbc49e4292730d5b1c77d26b4c03d7fad6/docs/tradezella-ui-tour.md)). It is a finance ledger, not a trade importer — "PropFirm Sync is bank fees and payouts, not trade import," as a competitor's comparison puts it ([Deltalytix](https://github.com/hugodemenez/deltalytix/blob/6c2bff8152a5b84ef2d7c7034373769b93196737/content/compare/en/tradezella.mdx)) — and because all ten auto-detected firms trade futures, it is positioned as a free acquisition hook for the futures-prop audience that then feeds the multi-account upsell to Pro and Ultra.

## Rails, React and 16 real auto-sync connectors sit behind the "500+ brokers" claim

Alpaca and Tradier sync are "coming soon" ([Help: Alpaca](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/14487321-alpaca-broker-sync-coming-soon); [Help: Tradier](https://intercom.help/tradezella-4066d388d93c/en/articles/9628452-tradier-broker-support-coming-soon)), Binance, Kraken and Saxo are absent entirely, and users have written their own bridges — a Chrome extension that converts Saxo Excel history into the generic CSV on the upload page and a Python script for Bitunix fills ([Saxo extension](https://github.com/vxcall/saxo-tradezella/blob/3f7bbba2fb84d610bbf5b3c5f2da819ce19fcb21/README.md); [Bitunix script](https://github.com/kaifri/BitunixTradezellaSync/blob/dc2f5716280efd35f7dc0b7cca90420befd40ea0/bitunix_to_tradezella.py)). A Canny Integrations board collects requests (Zerodha, AMP Futures, Capital.com, Merrill, CQG Desktop, TradingView Strategy Tester) alongside 352 feature requests ([Canny](https://tradezella.canny.io/); [Canny Integrations](https://tradezella.canny.io/integrations)).

Auto-sync is built broker by broker with heterogeneous credential models, not through a consumer aggregator; a SnapTrade-based competitor lists TradeZella itself as "CSV-only" ([market-track-flow](https://github.com/lidor656/market-track-flow/blob/c48a78b2b4ec89c1f89a83f1f37963b66ba4bc5d/src/hooks/useSnapTradeBrokers.ts)). Interactive Brokers uses a Flex Web Service token whose activation period must be set to 365 days, with same-day trades lagging about a day so CSV is advised for immediacy ([Help: IBKR troubleshooting](https://intercom.help/tradezella-4066d388d93c/en/articles/6082692-troubleshooting-interactive-brokers-ibkr-sync-issue)); Charles Schwab/thinkorswim broke when the TD Ameritrade API retired, requiring a reconnect that selects all accounts ([Help: thinkorswim update 13-05-24](https://intercom.help/tradezella-4066d388d93c/en/articles/9318278-thinkorswim-sync-issues-update-13-05-24)); Tradovate sometimes classifies live accounts under demo credentials and its data omits commissions ([Help: Tradovate](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/9557659-tradovate-how-to-sync-your-tradovate-account-with-tradezella)); TradeStation connects live accounts only and shares about 90 days of history ([Help: TradeStation](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/6739435-tradestation-how-to-sync-your-tradestation-account-with-tradezella)); cTrader uses an OAuth-style "Allow access" popup ([Help: cTrader](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/9562964-ctrader-how-to-sync-your-ctrader-account-with-tradezella)); Rithmic is "a direct integration with the R | Protocol API" using firm-issued credentials for Apex, Topstep, Bulenox and others ([Help: Rithmic](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/16074943-rithmic-r-trader-how-to-sync-your-rithmic-r-trader-account-with-tradezella)); TopstepX requires a paid ProjectX API subscription and key ([Help: TopstepX](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/13853592-topstepx-how-to-sync-your-topstepx-account-with-tradezella)); and MetaTrader uses the broker's server name plus the read-only investor password. TradeZella therefore stores broker credentials server-side for several connectors with no public statement on encryption; sync frequency is undocumented (competitors claim "every 3 hours"), the dashboard shows "Last import … Resync," and the IBKR path is inherently end-of-day. Trade construction is position-based: a trade is the sequence of executions between flat states per account and symbol, with a running Position column, opposite-side defaults, Intraday/Multiday classification, exit-day P&L attribution and manual split and merge ([UI tour](https://github.com/0xsickre/Trading_Journal/blob/eee601fbc49e4292730d5b1c77d26b4c03d7fad6/docs/tradezella-ui-tour.md); [Help: split or merge](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/7031683-how-can-i-split-or-merge-a-trade)); the FIFO-versus-average-cost policy, duplicate detection on re-upload, multi-leg option grouping and FX conversion to a base currency are all undocumented.

Outbound data is deliberately thin. The only export is the Trade View CSV, trade-level rather than fill-level, named `trades_YYYYMMDDHHMMSS.csv`, with no full backup and no re-import ([Help: export](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/9725069-how-to-export-data-to-a-csv-file-from-the-trade-log-page)); competitors' migration importers reconstruct one entry and one exit at average prices and fold any difference into fees because "an average-price trade export cannot recover original partial fills or the intratrade price path" ([LuxAlgo importer](https://github.com/LuxAlgo/trade-journal/blob/834e7b68f553d1aba0d7777875cdc4622f5a7eb5/packages/importers/src/formats/tradezella.ts)), and the header they parse is Account Name, Open/Close Date and Time, Instrument, Side, Quantity, Entry/Exit Price, Gross P&L, Commission, Duration, Adjusted Cost and Adjusted Proceeds, with Setups and Mistakes riding along ([Deltalytix parser](https://github.com/hugodemenez/deltalytix/blob/6c2bff8152a5b84ef2d7c7034373769b93196737/lib/import-parsers/tradezella.ts)). There is **no public API, webhook, Zapier connector or TradingView-alert ingestion**; public share links of the form `app.tradezella.com/shared/{accountHash}/trade/{tradeHash}`, emailed report subscriptions, Spaces and the new MCP server are the only ways data leaves. Security claims are marketing-grade — the FAQ says "you own your data … and we are 100% secure" ([Contact](https://www.tradezella.com/contact-us)) — and no SOC 2 or ISO 27001 attestation appears anywhere, while a directory lists no certifications ([SpotSaaS](https://www.spotsaas.com/product/tradezella)). The implied relational model is User → Accounts (timezone, balance adjustments, commission rules) → Trades (instrument type, side, status, reviewed, rating, planned stop and target, strategy, notes) → Executions (timestamp, quantity, price, multiplier, commission, fee, swap, running position, adjusted cost and proceeds); Trade ↔ Tags with Tag → Category; Strategy → RuleGroups → Rules with a show-when enum plus per-trade rule check-offs; Notes → Folder with optional Template and a polymorphic link to a trade or day; ProgressRule → daily rule results; attachments on trades and day sessions; backtest and replay scenarios referencing strategies; PropFirmAccount → Transactions and Payouts; and Spaces for sharing — with MAE/MFE, best exit, running P&L, Zella Scale and Zella Insights computed by an asynchronous enrichment pipeline that joins executions to the price store.

## Record derivatives volume and prop-firm rules drive demand while new-trader inflow shrinks

The journal's addressable core is the high-frequency, derivatives-active retail trader, and that segment set records in 2025. OCC cleared **15,207,163,554 options contracts in 2025 against 12,224,227,606 in 2024, up 24.4%**, with equity options up 26.8% ([OCC](https://www.theocc.com/newsroom/views/2026/01-05-occ-annual-2025-and-december-2025-volume)); Cboe calls it the sixth consecutive record year with roughly 61 million contracts a day and the first 100-million-contract day on April 4, 2025 ([Cboe](https://www.cboe.com/insights/posts/the-state-of-the-options-industry-2025)). CME's Micro E-mini equity futures set a single-day record of 8.1 million contracts on April 7, 2025 and a Q2 2025 average of more than 3.4 million a day, up 43% year over year ([CME Group](https://www.cmegroup.com/newsletters/quarterly-equity-index-recap/2025-july-equity-index-recap.html)); CME says it works with 130+ retail brokers and has grown retail traders on its platform to **over 500,000 from about 150,000 five years earlier** ([FOW](https://www.fow.com/insights/analysis-cme-targets-growing-retail-demand-for-futures-options)), and it launched E-nano equity futures at one-tenth the Micro size on August 24, 2026 explicitly to "expand retail access" ([CME Group](https://www.cmegroup.com/media-room/press-releases/2026/8/03/cme_group_to_expandretailaccesstofourleadingbenchmarkswithe-nano.html)). Retail ran 20–25% of US equity volume with a record 35% spike during the April 2025 tariff volatility ([Benzinga](https://cdn4.benzinga.com/markets/tech/26/01/49671587/retail-investors-historic-2025-streak-record-inflows-record-influence)), and Robinhood reported 26.8 million funded customers and $20.4 billion of net deposits in Q3 2025 ([Robinhood](https://investors.robinhood.com/news-releases/news-release-details/robinhood-reports-third-quarter-2025-results)). The counter-signal is cohort exhaustion: FINRA Foundation's 2024 investor survey found **only 8% of investors had begun within the prior two years, versus 21% in 2021**, with under-35 participation down from 32% to 26% and willingness to take substantial risk down from 12% to 8% ([ThinkAdvisor](https://www.thinkadvisor.com/amp/2025/12/05/pandemic-era-surge-of-new-investors-is-over/)). The same survey describes the core persona: under-35 investors of whom 43% trade options and 22% use margin, who get information from YouTube and of whom 61% follow finfluencer recommendations ([FundsSociety](https://www.fundssociety.com/en/news/mercados/fewer-new-investors-and-a-decline-among-young-people/)). Demand is therefore intensifying (more trades per trader, more complex products) rather than widening, it co-moves with volatility episodes, and founder-led and influencer distribution is structurally efficient for this buyer.

The prop-firm ("funded trader") industry is the second demand engine and is almost entirely unaudited. An analytics vendor estimates segment revenue at **about $585 million in 2025 rising 45% to about $850 million in 2026**, notes that no top-five firm publishes audited trader counts ([Track360](https://track360.io/blog/prop-trading-industry-report-2026-market-analysis)), and some 200+ firms operate globally; FTMO's acquisition of the broker OANDA closed on December 1, 2025 ([FX News Group](https://fxnewsgroup.com/forex-news/retail-forex/prop-firm-ftmo-closes-acquisition-of-cfds-broker-oanda/)). Every evaluation carries hard rules — daily loss limits, trailing drawdown, consistency rules — whose violation forfeits the fee, which is the single clearest mechanical driver of demand for rule-tracking tools like PropFirm Sync, and it is why TradeZella, its affiliates and its competitors all publish prop-firm rankings. The sector's regulatory footing is unsettled: the CFTC's flagship case against My Forex Funds was dismissed with prejudice on May 13, 2025 because of the agency's own misconduct ([LiquidityFinder](https://liquidityfinder.com/news/myforexfunds-secures-dismissal-of-cftc-case-after-judge-finds-misleading-statements-76fa6)), and US firms are voluntarily registering with the CFTC and NFA while the agency has made no statement on the evaluation model ([Finance Magnates](https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/us-prop-firms-are-now-moving-inside-the-cftc-perimeter-an-opportunity-or-a-survival-strategy/)), so a journal whose growth leans on prop integrations carries a reputational dependency it should diversify.

Why a $30–60 monthly journal sells at all is answered by the behavioural-finance base rates, not by evidence that journaling works. Among Brazilians who began day trading equity-index futures in 2013–2015 and persisted at least 300 days, **97% lost money** ([FGV repository](https://repositorio.fgv.br/items/e87d04fc-4b4c-4a56-ab5a-1c84c1afde1b/full); [Quantpedia](https://quantpedia.com/retail-day-trading-is-an-uphill-battle/)); in Taiwan, aggregate day-trader performance is negative and 74% of day-trading volume comes from traders with a history of losses ([Barber, Lee, Liu, Odean and Zhang](https://faculty.haas.berkeley.edu/odean/papers/day%20traders/day%20trading%20and%20learning%20110217.pdf)); mandatory EU/UK broker disclosures compiled across 49 brokers average a 71.1% retail CFD loss rate ([BrokerRank](https://brokerrank.net/research/retail-loss-rates)). No peer-reviewed study shows that trade journaling improves returns; the mechanism is supported only indirectly, by an experimental market in which the self-monitoring personality trait improved trading performance ([Biais, Hilton, Mazurier and Pouget](https://www.tse-fr.eu/sites/default/files/medias/doc/wp/2004/psycho.pdf)) and by a 138-experiment meta-analysis in which monitoring goal progress improved attainment (d = 0.40), cited via a vendor that concedes no rigorous trading study exists ([Glanwick](https://glanwick.com/why-journaling)). Vendor figures such as "73% better results" have no traceable source ([TradeInsights](https://tradeinsights.net/benefits-of-a-trading-journal)). The product's honest value is a shorter feedback loop that makes rule violations visible; its emotional sell is membership in the profitable minority. That gap matters legally: the FTC extracted $3 million from Warrior Trading and $2.425 million from Raging Bull over earnings claims ([FTC](https://ftc.gov/enforcement/refunds/warrior-trading-refunds); [Fox Business](https://foxbusiness.com/markets/raging-bull-customers-see-2-4m-refunds-ftc-dings-online-stock-trading-site)), and its June 29, 2023 Endorsement Guides treat testimonials of specific results as claims about typical results and render "results not typical" disclaimers ineffective ([Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/unitedstates/social-media/1337290/new-endorsement-guides-include-big-changes-but-few-surprises)). A journal marketed as "performance improvement" with winning-trader testimonials, or co-marketed with educators who make earnings claims, imports that liability; "process and discipline software" — what you did versus your plan — is the defensible positioning and happens to match the evidence. Structurally, churn is capped by the trader's own lifetime, since most new day traders quit within a year or two, which is why annual prepay, prop-firm re-engagement at each new evaluation, and education and community bundles are the levers every vendor pulls; and because the fresh-aspirant pool is shrinking, acquisition costs through YouTube and affiliates will rise, pushing the category toward retention and B2B channels such as brokers and prop firms embedding journaling.

The strategic reading follows from the competitive and market sections. No incumbent combines native mobile, reliable sync with transparent error states, account-level risk metrics such as equity drawdown, proactive behavioural AI, a real free tier or trial and a published refund policy at or below $20 a month; TradeZella's July 2026 move up-market and its metered AI widen that opening for beginners and low-volume traders, while the futures and prop-firm persona — the highest-intent, highest-ARPU segment and the one TradeZella markets to most directly — can be served by a specialist with fewer, better connectors (Rithmic, Tradovate, ProjectX) and a payouts-and-rules ledger. Whichever position capitals takes, the defensible claims are about process (rules followed, trades reviewed, time to review), the distribution plan has to exist before the feature list does, and the engineering budget should assume that connectors, market data and support, not dashboards, are where TradeZella's money goes.

## Conclusion

The durable lesson in TradeZella is that a trading journal is a trust business wearing an analytics costume. The company could hold prices flat for four years, skip free trials, and still grow because a 900K-subscriber founder, a creator network paid on recurring revenue, and educators who need their students on the same platform pre-sold the product before anyone saw a dashboard; the software's job was to not break that trust, and the record of where it did break — sync failures surfacing as missing trades, a mobile promise left open for two years, an AI that one user says corrupted 200 trades — maps exactly onto the complaint themes and onto the design corrections that followed, from reversible import batches to an MCP scope that never touches execution data. The July 2026 repricing marks a second phase: with a cost base now carrying 1-second market data, LLM agents and a compiled backtesting engine, TradeZella is monetizing intensity (more accounts, more replays, more runs, more credits) from a shrinking pool of new aspirants, which pushes it up-market and leaves the entry tier of the category structurally open.

For capitals, the implication is to invert TradeZella's sequence rather than copy its surface. Copy the ledger, the metric definitions, the review primitives and the daily commit ritual, because those are proven and cheap; treat market data, backtesting and AI as deliberate later commitments, because they are where the money and the data-integrity risk sit; and decide the distribution model first, because the one feature TradeZella has that cannot be rebuilt is its audience. The market evidence says the honest promise is faster recognition of what a trader actually did against what they planned, and the regulatory evidence says that is also the only promise safe to advertise.
